Canadian philosopher, artist, and dancer working on movement, neurodiversity, and research-creation. Founder of the SenseLab at Concordia University. Key works include *Relationscapes* (2009), *Always More Than One* (2013), *The Minor Gesture* (2016), and *Thought in the Act* (2014, with Massumi). Develops concepts including "worlding," "the interval," and "minor gesture." Theorizes movement as thinking rather than representation of thought. Central to Author's framework: the workshop as space of relational becoming.
