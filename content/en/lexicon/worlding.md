Whitehead/Manning's concept of creating space through movement rather than occupying pre-existing space. World as ongoing process rather than container: "worlding" names the activity by which entities co-constitute the environments they inhabit. Not world as given but world as made—and continuously remade—through relational activity. Every workshop is a worlding: participants create the space of encounter through their interactions.

**References:**

- MANNING, Erin. *The Minor Gesture*. Durham: Duke University Press, 2016.
