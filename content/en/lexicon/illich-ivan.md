Austrian philosopher and priest (1926-2002). *Tools for Conviviality* (1973) distinguished "convivial" tools that enhance human autonomy from "manipulative" tools that reduce users to operators. *Deschooling Society* (1971) argued that institutional education disables learning by creating dependency on credentials. Illich's critique of industrial institutions anticipates concerns about platform capitalism and surveillance: when tools scale beyond human comprehension, they dominate rather than serve.
