Russian term (self-publishing) for clandestine copying and distribution networks in Soviet-era Eastern Europe. Manuscripts were typed with carbon copies, each copy passed hand-to-hand, retyped when worn. The samizdat network constituted a parallel public sphere outside state control. Historical precedent for shadow libraries: decentralized, volunteer-maintained, operating through trust networks rather than institutions. Demonstrates that knowledge commons have always required practices that exceed legal authorization.
