Cage's event at Black Mountain College (summer 1952), often considered the first "happening." Simultaneous, uncoordinated performances: Cage on a ladder reading a lecture, Cunningham dancing, Rauschenberg's *White Paintings* hung from the ceiling, Tudor at the piano, M.C. Richards and Charles Olson reading poetry. No predetermined score coordinated the elements; chance determined timing. Demonstration of proto-computational aesthetics: distributed system without central control, emergence from simultaneous independent processes.
