Russian collective (founded 2003) combining art, theory, and activism. Named after Chernyshevsky's novel (Lenin's title). Based in Saint Petersburg, core members include Dmitry Vilensky, Olga Egorova (Tsaplya), Nikolay Oleynikov. Produces newspaper, video works, "learning plays" (after Brecht), and collective actions. Addresses post-Soviet condition, precarious labor, and possibilities for emancipatory politics. Demonstrates how collective practice can maintain both artistic rigor and political commitment.
