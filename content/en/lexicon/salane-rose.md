American artist working with archival materials and institutional systems. Notable for work with New York MTA (Metropolitan Transportation Authority) lost-and-found objects—items that enter bureaucratic limbo before eventual disposal. Practice investigates how institutions process loss, what falls through classificatory systems, and how objects circulate after their intended use-life. Relevant to Author's interest in entropic archives and the generative potential of archival gaps.

**References:**
- SALANE, Rose. "Objects Awaiting Classification." Whitney Biennial, 2022.
- SALANE, Rose. *Outstanding Wages*. New York: Karma Books, 2023.
