Belgian philosopher of science. *Cosmopolitics* (7 vols, 1996-1997; English 2010-2011) proposes "cosmopolitical" approach where all parties—including nonhumans—participate in constructing common worlds. With Philippe Pignarre, *Capitalist Sorcery* (2005) analyzes capitalism as capture system requiring "practices of disenchantment." *In Catastrophic Times* (2015) addresses Anthropocene with neither despair nor techno-optimism. Key for understanding how heterogeneous collectives might compose without homogenization.

**Key Concepts:** Cosmopolitics, Practices of Disenchantment, Ecology of Practices
