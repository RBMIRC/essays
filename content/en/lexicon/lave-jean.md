American anthropologist whose work on learning and everyday practice challenged cognitivist models of knowledge transmission. With Etienne Wenger, developed the concept of "legitimate peripheral participation" in *Situated Learning* (1991). Earlier work including *Cognition in Practice* (1988) demonstrated that mathematical reasoning in everyday contexts differs fundamentally from school-learned procedures. Learning is not internalization of abstract knowledge but transformation of participation in communities of practice.
