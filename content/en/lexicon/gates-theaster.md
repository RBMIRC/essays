American artist (b. 1973) whose practice extends from ceramics to urban regeneration, transforming abandoned buildings on Chicago's South Side into cultural infrastructure. Founder of the Rebuild Foundation; acquired and operates the Stony Island Arts Bank. Work operates at intersection of art, architecture, and community development without collapsing into any single discipline. Gates's BMC connection: his intensive workshops at the Rebuild Foundation echo BMC's integration of art, craft, and daily life; his emphasis on skill transmission and material practice resonates with Albers's Werklehre.

**References:**
- GATES, Theaster. *My Labor Is My Protest*. London: White Cube, 2012.
