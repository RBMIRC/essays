British philosopher (b. 1962), controversial founder of accelerationism. At Warwick's Cybernetic Culture Research Unit (CCRU) in the 1990s, developed "dark enlightenment" and theorized capitalism as autonomous machine beyond human control. *Fanged Noumena* (2011) collects his explosive theoretical writings. Accelerationist thesis: don't resist capitalism—accelerate it to breaking point. Later turned toward neo-reaction, splitting accelerationism into left (Srnicek/Williams) and right variants. Influential on speculative realism, techno-theory, and continental philosophy's engagement with computation.

**Key Concepts:** Accelerationism, Meltdown, Machinic Desire
