Hirschhorn's concept of authorship that remains singular while enabling collective participation. In works like *Gramsci Monument* and *Musée Précaire Albinet*, Hirschhorn maintains full authorship (unshared) while creating conditions for community involvement. Not collaboration (which distributes authorship) but a specific form of artistic responsibility: the artist authors the structure within which others act freely. Resolves the paradox of participatory art without sacrificing artistic vision.

**References:**
- HIRSCHHORN, Thomas. "Doing Art Politically: What Does This Mean?" In *Critical Laboratory: The Writings of Thomas Hirschhorn*, ed. Benjamin Buchloh. Cambridge: MIT Press, 2013.
- BISHOP, Claire. *Artificial Hells: Participatory Art and the Politics of Spectatorship*. London: Verso, 2012.
