German-American artist and educator (1888-1976), brought Bauhaus pedagogy to Black Mountain College (1933-1949). Developed *Werklehre*—learning through direct engagement with materials, approaching them with questions rather than plans. *Interaction of Color* (1963) codified his teaching method: color perception is relational, not absolute. Albers's pedagogy demonstrates embodied fallibilism: the material resists, the student adjusts, knowledge emerges through doing rather than instruction. Anti-theoretical yet rigorous; suspicious of verbal explanation, insistent on visual demonstration.
