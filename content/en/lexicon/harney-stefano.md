British theorist working on logistics, finance, and the undercommons. With Fred Moten, developed the concept of the "undercommons" in *The Undercommons* (2013)—fugitive spaces of study beneath institutional structures. Later work including *All Incomplete* (2021) extends these ideas through logistics and supply chain analysis. The university appears as site of debt and professionalization; the undercommons as space of study that refuses these captures. Against the "critical academic"; for the "subversive intellectual."
