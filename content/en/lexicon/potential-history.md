Ariella Aïsha Azoulay's concept for recovering possibilities foreclosed by imperial violence. Against history as what happened (already determined), potential history attends to what might have been, what was prevented, what remains latent. Imperial archives document conquest as inevitable; potential history reads against this grain to recover worlds that were destroyed but whose traces remain. Not counterfactual speculation but ethical refusal to accept imperial outcomes as final.
