Mierle Laderman Ukeles's concept declaring all maintenance and care work as art. "Manifesto for Maintenance Art" (1969) responded to the invisibility of domestic and sanitation labor: "I am an artist. I am a woman. I am a wife. I am a mother. (Random order). I do a hell of a lot of washing, cleaning, cooking, renewing, supporting, preserving, etc." Maintenance art makes visible the labor that sustains social life but remains systematically devalued. Foundation for social practice and for understanding care as constitutive of any commons.
