Large model trained on broad data, adaptable to many downstream tasks through fine-tuning or prompting. Term introduced by Stanford HAI (2021). GPT-4, Claude, LLaMA are foundation models for language; Stable Diffusion for images. Concentrates power in organizations able to afford training (tens of millions of dollars). Democratizes access through APIs while centralizing capability. The "foundation" metaphor suggests stability but these models encode particular data distributions and biases.
