French literary theorist and media scholar. *The Ecology of Attention* (2014) analyzes attention as scarce resource exploited by platform capitalism and proposes collective reclamation. *Mythocratie* (2010) examines narrative's political power. With Randy Martin, develops concept of "attentional derivatives"—how captured attention becomes financialized. Key for understanding how attention economy shapes knowledge production and pedagogical possibility.

**Key Concepts:** Ecology of Attention, Attentional Derivatives, Mythocratie
