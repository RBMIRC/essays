British theorist and artist (b. 1966), co-founder of The Otolith Group. *More Brilliant than the Sun* (1998) theorizes Afrofuturism through Detroit techno and electronic music. Developed "conceptechnics"—fusion of concept and sonic technique where sampling becomes philosophical method. With Anjalika Sagar as The Otolith Group, produces essay films exploring chronopolitics and speculative documentation. Key figure for theorizing how Black Atlantic sonic practices constitute alternative temporalities and epistemologies.
