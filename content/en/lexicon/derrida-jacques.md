French philosopher (1930-2004) whose analysis of 'archive fever' and the spectral dimensions of preservation informs the project's understanding of AI-generated images as archival phantasms. Key concepts include [[Archive Fever]], [[Spectral]], [[Différance]], and [[Trace]].

**Key Works:** *Archive Fever* (1995), *Specters of Marx* (1993)
