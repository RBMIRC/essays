French artist (b. 1962) whose work investigates temporality, living systems, and the construction of situations. *Untilled* (dOCUMENTA 13, 2012) created an ecosystem in a compost site with plants, animals, and a reclining nude sculpture with a beehive head. Work refuses the event-structure of exhibitions in favor of ongoing processes that exceed human control. Relevant to Author's concerns with time, entropy, and non-human agency; represents a post-relational practice where encounter happens with systems rather than communities.
