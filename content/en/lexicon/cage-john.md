American composer (1912-1992), central figure at Black Mountain College. *Theatre Piece No. 1* (1952) at BMC is considered the first "happening"—simultaneous, uncoordinated performances by Cage, Cunningham, Rauschenberg, Tudor, and others. Developed chance operations and indeterminacy as compositional methods. *4'33"* (1952) reframes silence as sound, listening as composition. Cage's BMC work established aleatory method as rigorous practice: not absence of structure but structure that includes the unplanned. Transmitted I Ching methodology to visual artists.

**Key Concepts:** Chance operations, Indeterminacy, Silence
