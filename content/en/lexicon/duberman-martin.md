American historian (b. 1930). *Black Mountain: An Exploration in Community* (1972) remains definitive history of BMC, based on extensive interviews with participants. Founded Center for Lesbian and Gay Studies (CLAGS) at CUNY. Work combines rigorous historical scholarship with personal engagement; BMC study reflects his own concerns with experimental community and sexual liberation. Essential source for understanding BMC's daily life, conflicts, and educational experiments.

**References:**
- DUBERMAN, Martin. *Black Mountain: An Exploration in Community*. New York: E.P. Dutton, 1972.
- DUBERMAN, Martin. *Cures: A Gay Man's Odyssey*. New York: Dutton, 1991.
