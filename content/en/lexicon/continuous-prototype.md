Heikkinen, Kaverma, and Ziegler's concept from design research: the work that remains permanently in prototype stage, never reaching "finished" status. Against the teleology of product development (prototype → production), the continuous prototype treats incompleteness as constitutive. Each iteration opens new possibilities rather than converging on final form. Resonates with Author's emphasis on process over product and with the workshop as space of ongoing making rather than completion.
