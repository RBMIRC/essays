When a model learns training data too precisely, memorizing specific examples rather than generalizing patterns. The model performs well on training data but poorly on new inputs. Opposite of underfitting (failing to capture patterns). Regularization techniques (dropout, weight decay) combat overfitting. Relevant to copyright debates: an overfitted model might reproduce training data verbatim, raising infringement concerns. The line between "learning style" and "memorizing content" is not technically clear.
