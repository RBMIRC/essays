French philosopher whose work addresses politics, aesthetics, and pedagogy through the concept of equality. *The Ignorant Schoolmaster* (1987) recovers Joseph Jacotot's radical pedagogy, arguing that intellectual emancipation proceeds from the assumption of equal intelligence. *The Emancipated Spectator* (2008) extends this to aesthetics: the spectator is already active, already interpreting. Against the "stultifying" logic that positions the master as knowing and the student as ignorant, Rancière insists on the equality of intelligences as starting point rather than goal.

**Key Concepts:** [[ignorant-schoolmaster|Ignorant Schoolmaster]], Equality of Intelligences, Distribution of the Sensible
