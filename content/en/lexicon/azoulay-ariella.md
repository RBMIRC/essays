Israeli-American cultural theorist and filmmaker. *Potential History: Unlearning Imperialism* (2019) proposes "unlearning" as method—refusing the imperial categories that structure archives and knowledge. Argues that archives are not neutral repositories but apparatuses of imperial violence; what they preserve is inseparable from what they destroy. Develops concept of "potential history": recovering possibilities foreclosed by imperial narratives. Essential for Author's critique of archival neutrality and for understanding archives as sites of ongoing political contestation.

**Key Concepts:** Unlearning Imperialism, Potential History, Imperial Shutter
