American feminist theorist and physicist. *Meeting the Universe Halfway* (2007) develops "agential realism" from quantum physics: entities do not pre-exist their interactions but emerge through "intra-action." Critique of representationalism: knowledge does not mirror pre-given world but participates in worldmaking. "Diffraction" as method (from Haraway) attending to patterns of difference rather than reflection's sameness. Essential for understanding how observation participates in constituting what it observes—relevant to archival and computational practices.

**Key Concepts:** Agential Realism, Intra-action, [[diffraction-method|Diffraction]]

**References:**
- BARAD, Karen. *Meeting the Universe Halfway: Quantum Physics and the Entanglement of Matter and Meaning*. Durham: Duke University Press, 2007.
