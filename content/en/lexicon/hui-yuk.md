Hong Kong philosopher (1985-) proposing 'cosmotechnics' as framework for thinking technology beyond Western universalism, central to the project's methodology. Key concepts include [[Cosmotechnics]], [[Technodiversity]], [[Recursivity]], and [[Digital Objects]].

**Key Works:** *The Question Concerning Technology in China* (2016), *Recursivity and Contingency* (2019), *Art and Cosmotechnics* (2021)
