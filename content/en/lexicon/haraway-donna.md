American scholar (1944-) whose concept of situated knowledge and diffractive methodology provides tools for thinking ethics in computational systems. Key concepts include [[Situated Knowledge]], [[Cyborg]], [[Diffraction]], and [[String Figures]].

**Key Works:** *Simians, Cyborgs, and Women* (1991), *Staying with the Trouble* (2016)
