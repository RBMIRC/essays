American filmmaker and artist (1927-1984) who studied at Black Mountain College and later pioneered computer-generated film. *Poemfields* series (1966-1969, with Ken Knowlton) used BEFLIX programming language to create abstract animations. Movie-Drome concept proposed immersive image environments. Developed early motion-capture systems and collaborated on LifeForms software used by Merce Cunningham. VanDerBeek represents the direct transmission of BMC's experimental ethos into computational aesthetics—chance operations become algorithmic procedures.
