British-Nigerian artist (b. 1995) creating video games, installations, and archives centered on Black trans experience. Work uses interactive digital formats to preserve stories otherwise excluded from official archives. *SHE KEEPS ME DAMN ALIVE* (2021) and the ongoing Black Trans Archive project use game engines and 3D environments as archival infrastructure. Represents a generation using computational tools for counter-archival practice, relevant to Author's interest in archives that generate rather than merely preserve.
