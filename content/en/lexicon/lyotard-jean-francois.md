French philosopher (1924-1998). *The Postmodern Condition* (1979) famously defined postmodernity as "incredulity toward metanarratives"—collapse of grand legitimating stories (progress, emancipation, totality). *The Differend* (1983) analyzes incommensurable phrase regimes where one party cannot articulate injury in available discourse. *Libidinal Economy* (1974) more delirious engagement with desire and capitalism. Central to debates on modernity/postmodernity; introduced "postmodern" into philosophical discourse.

**Key Concepts:** Metanarrative, Differend, Phrase Regimes, Postmodern Condition
