French art historian and philosopher. *Confronting Images* (1990) critiques iconology's reduction of images to texts. *The Surviving Image* (2002) develops Warburg's *Nachleben* (afterlife of images): visual forms persist and transform across historical periods, carrying affective charges. *Images in Spite of All* (2003) defends four photographs from Auschwitz against claims of unrepresentability. Key for understanding images as having their own temporality, rhythm, and agency beyond what they depict.

**Key Concepts:** [[nachleben|Nachleben]], Surviving Image, Anachronism
