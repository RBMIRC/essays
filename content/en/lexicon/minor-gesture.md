Manning's concept (2016) for gestures that alter the field without claiming major status. The minor does not become major but changes the conditions under which the major operates. Drawing on Deleuze/Guattari's "minor literature," Manning emphasizes how autistic perception and neurodiverse modes of attention reveal dimensions the neurotypical misses. The minor gesture is catalytic: it activates potentials without imposing form. Relevant to Author's emphasis on workshop practices that enable rather than determine.
