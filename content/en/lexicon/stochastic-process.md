Random process governed by probability laws; mathematical foundation for generative AI. Stochastic processes produce unpredictable individual outcomes while maintaining predictable aggregate distributions: any particular output is random, but the ensemble follows statistical patterns. Chance constrained by distribution. The tension between deterministic training and stochastic inference defines AI generation.
