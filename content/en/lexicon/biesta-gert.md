Dutch educational theorist critiquing the "learnification" of education—the reduction of teaching to facilitation of learning, measured through outcomes. *The Beautiful Risk of Education* (2013) argues that education's "weakness" (its inability to guarantee results) is constitutive: genuine education addresses subjects who may respond unpredictably. Against the culture of accountability and control; for education as existential event. Extends Deweyan pragmatism while questioning its progressive assumptions.

**Key Concepts:** [[beautiful-risk-of-education|Beautiful Risk of Education]], Learnification, Weakness of Education
