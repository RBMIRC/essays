British political theorist. With Nick Srnicek, co-authored the influential "#Accelerate: Manifesto for an Accelerationist Politics" (2013) and *Inventing the Future* (2015). Left accelerationism proposes that the left must abandon "folk politics" (localism, horizontalism, immediacy) and develop hegemonic projects that match capital's planetary scale. Not acceleration of capitalism itself but of the productive capacities capitalism has developed—repurposed for emancipatory ends.

**Key Concepts:** Left Accelerationism, Folk Politics, Counter-Hegemony
