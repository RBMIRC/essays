American artist, writer, and housing attorney. Co-founder with Camae Ayewa (Moor Mother) of Black Quantum Futurism (BQF) collective. Develops Afrofuturist temporal practices challenging linear time's colonial impositions. "Temporal ghettos" concept describes how racial capitalism confines Black communities to degraded temporal conditions. *Black Quantum Futurism: Theory and Practice* (2015) proposes alternative temporalities drawing on quantum physics and African diasporic traditions.
