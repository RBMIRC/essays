Network architecture where nodes communicate directly without central server. Each peer is both client and server. BitTorrent, IPFS, and early file-sharing networks (Napster, Gnutella) use P2P. Resists censorship and single points of failure but complicates content moderation and accountability. Shadow libraries often rely on P2P for distribution. Embodies rhizomatic structure: no hierarchy, no center, knowledge circulating between equal peers. Infrastructure that materializes certain political possibilities.
