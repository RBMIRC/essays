American Marxist literary critic and theorist (b. 1934). *Postmodernism, or, The Cultural Logic of Late Capitalism* (1991) argues postmodernism is not a style but the cultural dominant of multinational capitalism—characterized by pastiche, depthlessness, and the waning of affect. "Cognitive mapping" names the political art adequate to this condition. *The Political Unconscious* (1981): "Always historicize!" Central for understanding cultural production under late capitalism and the challenges of representing totality.

**Key Concepts:** Late Capitalism, Cognitive Mapping, Pastiche, Depthlessness
