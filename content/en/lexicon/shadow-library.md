Informal digital repositories providing free access to academic texts, often in violation of copyright law. Examples include Library Genesis (LibGen), Sci-Hub, AAAAARG, and Monoskop. Dušan Barok taxonomizes shadow libraries by their focus: academic papers (Sci-Hub), books (LibGen), art theory (AAAAARG/Monoskop). They represent practical negation of enclosure: where publishers restrict access through paywalls, shadow libraries distribute. Not merely piracy but infrastructure—maintained by custodians who take responsibility for knowledge circulation the market has failed to provide.
