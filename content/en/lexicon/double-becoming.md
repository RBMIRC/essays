Deleuze/Guattari's concept of mutual alteration where both terms transform without one becoming the other. "The wasp becomes orchid and the orchid becomes wasp": not imitation or identification but parallel transformation. Neither term remains what it was; neither becomes what the other was. Foundational for understanding practitioner-territory relations in the Eden project.

**References:**
- DELEUZE, Gilles, and Félix GUATTARI. *A Thousand Plateaus*. Trans. Brian Massumi. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1987.
