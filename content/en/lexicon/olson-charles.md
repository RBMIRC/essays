American poet (1910-1970), rector of Black Mountain College (1951-1956). "Projective Verse" (1950) theorized open-field composition: breath as unit, typewriter as scoring instrument, the poem as energy transfer from poet to reader. *The Maximus Poems* (1960-1975) applied these principles at epic scale. Olson's BMC leadership intensified the college's emphasis on process over product: writing as physical act, pedagogy as shared investigation. His "proprioceptive poetics" grounds verse in the body's knowledge of itself.
