American mathematician (1894-1964), founder of cybernetics. *Cybernetics: Or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine* (1948) established the interdisciplinary field studying feedback, control, and information in systems biological, mechanical, and social. The BMC cybernetics seminar Wiener led with Natasha Goldowski (1948) represents direct contact between cybernetic theory and experimental arts. Key insight: information and feedback as universal principles applicable across domains—anticipating computational thinking.

**Key Concepts:** Cybernetics, Feedback, Control
