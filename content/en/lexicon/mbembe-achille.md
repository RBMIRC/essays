Cameroonian philosopher and political theorist. "Necropolitics" (2003) extends Foucault's biopolitics to analyze sovereignty's power to dictate who lives and who dies—particularly in colonial and postcolonial contexts. *Critique of Black Reason* (2013) examines how "the Black" was constructed as category through slavery and colonialism. *Brutalisme* (2020) analyzes contemporary forms of violence. Essential for understanding how power operates through management of death, not only life.

**Key Concepts:** Necropolitics, Critique of Black Reason, Brutalisme
