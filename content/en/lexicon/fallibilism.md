Pragmatist principle that all knowledge claims are provisional and subject to revision. Peirce's foundational insight that certainty is unattainable but inquiry can still progress. What survives critique of meliorism: experimentation without guarantee of improvement, inquiry without expectation of final answers. Fallibilism is meliorism minus the faith—the method without the metaphysics.
