Belgian sociologist of art and culture. *The Murmuring of the Artistic Multitude: Global Art, Politics and Post-Fordism* (2010) analyzes contemporary art's conditions under cognitive capitalism. *Institutional Attitudes: Instituting Art in a Flat World* (2013) examines how art institutions navigate between autonomy and instrumentalization. Work on "commonism" proposes alternatives to both market and state logics. Key for understanding how artistic practice operates within—and against—neoliberal institutional frameworks.

**Key Concepts:** Artistic Multitude, Commonism, Institutional Attitudes
