French philosopher specializing in John Dewey and democratic theory. *Participer: Essai sur les formes démocratiques de la participation* (2011) theorizes participation as tripartite: prendre part (taking part), apporter une part (contributing), and recevoir une part (benefiting). Against participation as mere presence; for participation as active contribution transforming both participant and collective. *Introduction à John Dewey* (2015) provides accessible entry to Dewey's thought. Essential for understanding participatory democracy and its pedagogical implications.

**Key Concepts:** Participation (tripartite), Democratic Inquiry, Contribution
