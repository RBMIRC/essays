Algorithmic generation of content through rules rather than manual creation; variation within constraint. Procedural generation produces difference from repetition: the same algorithm yields endless variants. Anticipates AI generation while differing structurally: procedural systems are deterministic (same seed, same output); AI systems are stochastic (same prompt, different outputs). BMC practices (chance operations, rule-based composition) were proto-procedural.
