*Author's term* (drawing on Syjuco) for archival practice that operates outside institutional authorization. The rogue archivist intervenes in existing archives rather than following established protocols: recontextualizing, annotating, revealing exclusions, redistributing. Not anti-archival (which would refuse archives altogether) but para-archival: working alongside and against institutional frameworks. Related to custodianship but emphasizes the interventionist dimension—the rogue archivist transforms what they handle.
