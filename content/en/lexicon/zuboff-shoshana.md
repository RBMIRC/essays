American scholar (1951-) whose analysis of surveillance capitalism reveals the economic infrastructure supporting AI's transformation of human experience into predictive commodities. Key concepts include [[Surveillance Capitalism]], [[Behavioral Surplus]], and [[Prediction Products]].

**Key Works:** *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism* (2019)
