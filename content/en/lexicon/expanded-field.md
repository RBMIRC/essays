Rosalind Krauss's concept ("Sculpture in the Expanded Field," 1979) mapping how sculpture expanded beyond its traditional definition through negation and combination. The "expanded field" includes not-landscape, not-architecture, and their combinations (site-construction, marked sites, axiomatic structures). Method of structural analysis that reveals how categories transform under pressure. Applicable beyond sculpture: the "archive in the expanded field" might include not-preservation, not-collection, and their recombinations.

**References:**
