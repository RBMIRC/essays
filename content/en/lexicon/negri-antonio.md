Italian Marxist philosopher (b. 1933). With Michael Hardt, authored *Empire* (2000), *Multitude* (2004), and *Commonwealth* (2009)—trilogy theorizing post-Fordist capitalism and possibilities for resistance. "Empire" names the new form of sovereignty: decentralized, networked, global. "Multitude" is the collective subject that might oppose it, irreducible to people or working class. Earlier autonomist work (*Marx Beyond Marx*, 1979) emphasized living labor's autonomy from capital. Central to post-operaismo and theories of immaterial labor.

**Key Concepts:** Empire, Multitude, Commonwealth, Immaterial Labor
