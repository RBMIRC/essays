American artist (b. 1981) working with photography, installation, and performance to investigate racial identity, passing, and the construction of American domestic space. Her staged self-portraits inhabit different personas, revealing the performativity of race and class. Work relevant to Author's interest in the spectral image and the question of what images testify to when the real they depict is itself a construction.
