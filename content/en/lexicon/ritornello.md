Deleuze/Guattari's concept of the refrain; gesture creating territory through repetition with difference. The ritornello establishes home while enabling departure: a child sings to create safe space; an artist repeats motifs to constitute a style; a bird's song marks territory. Not mere repetition but repetition that produces—each iteration slightly different, variation within consistency.
