Martinican philosopher (1928-2011) whose concept of opacity—the right not to be understood on the dominant's terms—provides ethical framework for engaging across difference. Key concepts include [[Opacity]], [[Relation]], and [[Creolization]].

**Key Works:** *Poetics of Relation* (1990)
