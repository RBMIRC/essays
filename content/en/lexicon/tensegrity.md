Fuller/Snelson's concept of structural integrity through continuous tension and discontinuous compression. Isolated compression elements (struts) are held in relation by a continuous tensional network (cables). The structure gains strength not from massive elements but from the distribution of forces. Snelson, a BMC student, developed the first tensegrity structure; Fuller named and theorized it. Proto-computational in emphasizing relations over elements.
