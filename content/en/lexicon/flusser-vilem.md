Czech-Brazilian philosopher (1920-1991) whose theory of technical images and the apparatus anticipates many features of AI image generation. Key concepts include [[Technical Image]], [[Apparatus]], [[Functionary]], [[Universe of Technical Images]], and [[Program]].

**Key Works:** *Towards a Philosophy of Photography* (1983), *Into the Universe of Technical Images* (1985)
