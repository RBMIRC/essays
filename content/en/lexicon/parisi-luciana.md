Italian philosopher (1967-) whose work on computation, abduction, and the continuum provides key conceptual resources for understanding AI's speculative operations. Key concepts include [[Abduction]], [[Symbiotic Architecture]], [[Incomputability]], and [[Critical Computation]].

**Key Works:** *Abstract Sex* (2004), *Contagious Architecture* (2013)
