Canadian philosopher working on affect, movement, and process philosophy. Translator of Deleuze and Guattari's *A Thousand Plateaus*. Key works include *Parables for the Virtual* (2002), *Semblance and Event* (2011), and *Couplets* (2024). Develops concepts including "quasi-causal technique," "field of belonging," and "elasticity of the almost." Extends Whitehead's process philosophy toward political and aesthetic questions. Central to Author's theoretical framework: affect as autonomous intensity, relation as ontologically primary.
