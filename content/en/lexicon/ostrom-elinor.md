American political economist (1933-2012), Nobel Prize in Economics (2009). *Governing the Commons* (1990) challenged the "tragedy of the commons" narrative, demonstrating through extensive fieldwork that communities can successfully manage shared resources without privatization or state control. Identified design principles for sustainable commons governance: clear boundaries, collective choice, monitoring, graduated sanctions. Essential framework for understanding how knowledge commons—including shadow libraries and open-source projects—can sustain themselves.

**Key Concepts:** Commons Governance, Design Principles, Polycentricity
