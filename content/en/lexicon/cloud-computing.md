Computing resources (storage, processing, services) accessed remotely over networks. Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure dominate. "The cloud" obscures material infrastructure: data centers consuming electricity, cooling systems, fiber optic cables, land use. Centralizes control while distributing access. AI training and inference increasingly cloud-dependent. Bratton's Stack theorizes cloud as geopolitical layer. The metaphor of immateriality conceals very material extraction and energy consumption.
