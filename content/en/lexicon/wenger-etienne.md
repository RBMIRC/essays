Swiss learning theorist who, with Jean Lave, developed the concepts of "legitimate peripheral participation" and "communities of practice" in *Situated Learning* (1991). Extended this work in *Communities of Practice* (1998), theorizing how groups develop shared practices, identities, and repertoires through sustained mutual engagement. Learning is not acquisition of knowledge but becoming—transformation of identity through participation in social practice.
