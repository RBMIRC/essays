Latour's concept of hybrid entities belonging to no single discipline; circulating between human and non-human, nature and culture. Quasi-objects are "real as nature, narrated as discourse, collective as society": they cannot be purified into any single category. AI-generated images are quasi-objects: neither purely technical nor purely cultural, neither natural nor artificial, but constitutively hybrid.

- LATOUR, Bruno. *We Have Never Been Modern*. Trans. Catherine Porter. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1993.
