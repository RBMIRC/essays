Swiss artist (b. 1957) whose participatory monuments and precarious museums exemplify "presence and production" as artistic method. Works including *Musée Précaire Albinet* (2004), *Gramsci Monument* (2013), and *Flamme éternelle* (2014) install makeshift structures in working-class neighborhoods, maintaining daily presence throughout. Developed concept of "unshared authorship"—maintaining full artistic responsibility while enabling community participation. Against relational aesthetics' dissolution of authorship; for intensive engagement over spectacular installation.
