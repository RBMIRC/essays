Mexican-American philosopher working on complexity theory, assemblage theory, and materialist ontology. *A New Philosophy of Society* (2006) develops Deleuze/Guattari's assemblage concept into systematic social theory. *Intensive Science and Virtual Philosophy* (2002) reconstructs Deleuzian ontology through mathematics and physics. Emphasizes emergence, nonlinear causality, and the role of intensive differences in generating extensive forms. Key for understanding social formations as dynamic assemblages rather than fixed structures.

**Key Concepts:** Assemblage Theory, Intensive/Extensive, Nonlinear Causality
