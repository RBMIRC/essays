French cultural theorist and urbanist (1932-2018). "Dromology"—the logic of speed—as key to understanding modern power. *Speed and Politics* (1977) argues that speed is the essence of war and politics. *The Vision Machine* (1988) analyzes automated perception. "The accident" as integral to every technology. Critic of cybernetics and digital networks; theorist of "polar inertia" where instantaneous communication eliminates space. Relevant to accelerationism debates and to understanding how velocity transforms experience.

**Key Concepts:** Dromology, Polar Inertia, The Accident, Vision Machine
