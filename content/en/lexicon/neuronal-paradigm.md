*Author's term* (after Busta) for the shift from linear to network-based cultural transmission. Where the linear paradigm transmitted contents through vertical channels (teacher to student, publisher to reader), the neuronal paradigm circulates by contagion, lateral connections, and statistical aggregation. AI systems make the neuronal paradigm visible: knowledge emerges not from individual transmission but from massive parallel processing.
