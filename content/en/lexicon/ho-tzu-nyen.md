Singaporean artist (b. 1976) working across film, installation, and performance to investigate Southeast Asian history, mythology, and colonial entanglement. *The Nameless* (2015) and *The Critical Dictionary of Southeast Asia* (ongoing) use AI and algorithmic processes to generate historical fabulations. Work demonstrates how computational methods can produce alternative historiographies, relevant to Author's concerns with archival speculation and the powers of the false.
