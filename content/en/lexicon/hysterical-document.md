Walid Raad's term for documents that behave symptomatically rather than transparently. In the Atlas Group project, "hysterical documents" are archives that manifest the impossibility of documenting traumatic historical events directly. The document's hysteria—its gaps, contradictions, fabrications—testifies more truthfully than apparent authenticity could. Neither fake (which implies a true original) nor documentary (which claims transparent reference): the hysterical document operates where evidence and imagination become indistinguishable.
