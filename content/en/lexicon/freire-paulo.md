Brazilian educator and philosopher (1921-1997). *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1968) theorized education as practice of freedom rather than transmission of knowledge. Critiqued "banking model" of education where teachers deposit information in passive students; proposed "problem-posing" education where teacher and student investigate the world together. Concept of "conscientization" (conscientização): critical awareness of social conditions as precondition for transformative action. Foundational for critical pedagogy and for understanding education as inherently political.
