French philosopher (1924-1989) whose theory of individuation and technical objects provides conceptual tools for understanding AI systems as processes rather than products. Key concepts include [[Individuation]], [[Transduction]], [[Preindividual]], [[Technical Mentality]], and [[Concretization]].

**Key Works:** *On the Mode of Existence of Technical Objects* (1958), *Individuation in Light of Notions of Form and Information* (1964)
