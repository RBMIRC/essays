Bourriaud's term (1998) for art practices that take human relations and social context as their medium rather than autonomous objects. Rirkrit Tiravanija cooking pad thai, Philippe Parreno organizing parties, Liam Gillick creating discussion platforms. Art as "state of encounter" producing intersubjective exchange. Contested by Bishop for privileging conviviality and consensus; defended as democratizing art's social function. Resonates with Author's interest in the workshop form while differing in emphasis on antagonism and maintained authorship (cf. Hirschhorn's "unshared authorship").
