Gregory Sholette's concept (*Dark Matter: Art and Politics in the Age of Enterprise Culture*, 2011) for the vast majority of artistic labor that remains invisible to the art world's attention economy. Failed artists, amateur practitioners, hobbyists, collectives, pedagogues—the "dark matter" whose gravitational pull shapes the art world without being seen. Like astronomical dark matter, it constitutes the majority of the field's mass while remaining undetected by conventional measures (sales, exhibitions, critical attention). Essential for understanding the labor that sustains artistic production.

**References:**
- SHOLETTE, Gregory. *Dark Matter: Art and Politics in the Age of Enterprise Culture*. London: Pluto Press, 2011.
- SHOLETTE, Gregory, and Matthew STIMSON, eds. *Collectivism after Modernism*. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2007.
