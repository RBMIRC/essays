American choreographer and dancer (1919-2009), formative presence at Black Mountain College. Developed chance-based choreography in collaboration with Cage; movement sequences determined by I Ching, coin tosses, or other aleatory methods. Separated dance from music: performers worked independently until performance. Founded the Merce Cunningham Dance Company (1953). Cunningham's method demonstrates how constraint enables rather than limits: chance operations generate movement the choreographer would not have invented, expanding the vocabulary of possibility.
