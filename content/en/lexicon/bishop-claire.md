British art historian and critic. *Artificial Hells: Participatory Art and the Politics of Spectatorship* (2012) provides critical history of participatory and socially engaged art. "Antagonism and Relational Aesthetics" (*October*, 2004) challenged Bourriaud's consensual model, arguing that genuine political art maintains conflict rather than resolving it. Distinguishes ethical evaluation (good intentions) from aesthetic evaluation (artistic quality). Essential for understanding debates around Hirschhorn, social practice, and the politics of participation.

**Key Concepts:** Antagonism, Artificial Hells, Delegated Performance
