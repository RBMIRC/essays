Harney and Moten's concept for spaces of study and sociality that exist beneath institutional structures. The undercommons is not a place but a practice: "study" as collective thinking that refuses professionalization and credentialing. Against the "critical academic" who seeks recognition within institutional hierarchies; for the "subversive intellectual" who works in the institution while refusing its logics. The undercommons names what happens in hallways, break rooms, informal gatherings—thought as fugitive sociality.
