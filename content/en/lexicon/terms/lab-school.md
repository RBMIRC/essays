Dewey's experimental school at University of Chicago (1896–1904); laboratory where pedagogical hypotheses were tested through inquiry, manual labor, and problem-solving rather than lecture. Students cooked, wove, built, cultivated—not as recreation but as modes of access to knowledge. The guiding principle was that all knowledge emerges from lived problematic situations. Direct precursor to BMC's educational philosophy: Rice radicalized Lab School principles by applying them to adults, adding art as central discipline, and anchoring them in residential community. Theory produces the school, the school transforms theory—a feedback loop that *retcon* formalizes.
