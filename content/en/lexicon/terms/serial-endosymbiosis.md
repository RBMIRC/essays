Margulis's theory of evolution through incorporation rather than competition; organisms evolve by integrating former parasites as organelles. Mitochondria were once independent bacteria; chloroplasts were once cyanobacteria. Evolution proceeds by symbiosis, merger, and cooperation—not only by selection and competition. Model for AI-human symbiosis: not replacement but incorporation, with both parties transformed.

**References:**
- MARGULIS, Lynn. *Symbiotic Planet: A New Look at Evolution*. New York: Basic Books, 1998.
- MARGULIS, Lynn, and Dorion SAGAN. *Acquiring Genomes: A Theory of the Origins of Species*. New York: Basic Books, 2002.
