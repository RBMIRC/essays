Glissant's concept of identity as constituted through encounter rather than essence; relational ontology where subjects emerge through contact with others. Opposes root-identity (single origin) with rhizome-identity (multiple connections). Central to understanding how the Eden project positions practitioners and residents as mutually constituting rather than pre-formed subjects meeting.

**References:**
- GLISSANT, Édouard. *Poetics of Relation*. Trans. Betsy Wing. Ann Arbor: University of Michigan Press, 1997.
