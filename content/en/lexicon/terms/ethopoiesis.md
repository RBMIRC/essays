Despret's concept (developed via Massumi) of self-inventive becoming of forms of life in relation. How organisms create new modes of existence through encounter—not adapting to pre-existing niches but inventing new ways of being. Ethopoiesis shifts focus from what beings are to how they become capable of new affects through relation.

**References:**
- DESPRET, Vinciane. "The Body We Care For: Figures of Anthropo-zoo-genesis." *Body & Society* 10, no. 2–3 (2004): 111–134.
- MASSUMI, Brian. *What Animals Teach Us about Politics*. Durham: Duke University Press, 2014.
