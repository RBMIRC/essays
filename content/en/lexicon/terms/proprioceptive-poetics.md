Olson's concept of poetry emphasizing bodily knowledge and breath; verse as extension of the poet's physiology onto the page. "The HEAD, by way of the EAR, to the SYLLABLE / the HEART, by way of the BREATH, to the LINE." Proprioceptive poetics makes the body's rhythms compositional principle: spacing, line breaks, and typography register physical experience. Proto-computational in treating the page as field rather than linear sequence.
