Poliks and Trillo's term (*Exocapitalism*, 2025) for computational operations that transform material into new configurations. Neither *lift* (extraction/departure) nor *drag* (parasitic resistance) but metamorphic recombination. The *fold* describes how the Eden project's archival infrastructure (Theodore, The Studies) transforms documentary material into speculative outputs—neither preserving nor destroying but reconfiguring through computational processes.

**References:**
- POLIKS, Marek, and Roberto Alonso TRILLO. *Exocapitalism: Economies with Absolutely No Limits*. Berlin/Nicosia: Becoming Press, 2025.
- DELEUZE, Gilles. *The Fold: Leibniz and the Baroque*. Trans. Tom Conley. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1993.
