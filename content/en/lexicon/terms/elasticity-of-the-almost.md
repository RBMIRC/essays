Manning's concept of microperception sensing possible steps before they occur; the felt potential of movement not yet taken but already shaping present action. The "almost" is elastic because it stretches toward multiple futures simultaneously, contracting only at the moment of actualization. Crucial for understanding improvisation in workshop contexts: participants perceive and respond to what is about to happen.

**References:**
- MANNING, Erin. *Relationscapes: Movement, Art, Philosophy*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2009.
- MANNING, Erin. *The Minor Gesture*. Durham: Duke University Press, 2016.
