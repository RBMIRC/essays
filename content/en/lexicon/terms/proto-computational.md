*Author's term* for structures anticipating computational aesthetics without computational machinery. BMC practices (weaving grids, chance operations, feedback composition, systems thinking) as pre-digital computation. Proto-computational does not mean "primitive" or "incomplete" but structurally homologous: the same abstract machines operating through different substrates. Weaving is computation in thread; programming is weaving in code.
