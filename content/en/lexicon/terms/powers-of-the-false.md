Deleuze's concept of fabulation producing narratives that relaunch reality; falsity as creative force rather than mere negation of truth. The "false" is not error but invention—the capacity to produce realities that did not exist before. Powers of the false operate in retcon: not correcting history but releasing its unrealized potential.

**References:**
- DELEUZE, Gilles. *Cinema 2: The Time-Image*. Trans. Hugh Tomlinson and Robert Galeta. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1989.
