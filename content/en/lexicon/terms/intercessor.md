Deleuze's concept of the catalyst that creates conditions for mutual becoming. Not mediator (standing between parties to facilitate exchange) but intercessor (intervening to transform both parties). The intercessor does not represent or translate but provokes—forcing each term to exceed itself through encounter. AI systems can function as intercessors in research-creation.

**References:**
- DELEUZE, Gilles. "Mediators" (1985). In *Negotiations*. Trans. Martin Joughin. New York: Columbia University Press, 1995.
