Fuller's systems thinking emphasizing geometric and energetic principles; whole-systems approach anticipating network theory. "Synergetics" names the study of systems whose behavior cannot be predicted from isolated components—only the whole exhibits the property. Fuller's geodesic structures embody synergetics: strength emerges from the network of relations, not from any single element. Proto-computational in its emphasis on emergent properties.

**References:**
