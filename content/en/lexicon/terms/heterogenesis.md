Guattari's concept of production of difference; becoming-other through encounter rather than reproduction of the same. Opposes homogenesis (production of sameness). In the Eden project, heterogenesis describes how practitioners and territory mutually transform through engagement—neither remains what it was before contact. The workshop produces heterogeneous outputs irreducible to either practitioner's prior practice or territory's given conditions.

**References:**
- GUATTARI, Félix. *Chaosmosis: An Ethico-Aesthetic Paradigm*. Trans. Paul Bains and Julian Pefanis. Bloomington: Indiana University Press, 1995.
