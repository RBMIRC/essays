Wielocha's concept of open-ended rhizomatic structure where artwork identity is distributed across activations. Neither fixed object nor pure event but ongoing process of becoming. The (An)archive resists closure: each activation adds to rather than reproduces the work. Distinct from traditional archive (preserving fixed records) and from pure performance (leaving no trace).
