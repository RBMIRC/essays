Lütticken's concept of staging futures not yet taken place; shift from reenactment (restaging past) to anticipatory performance of unrealized possibilities. Pre-enactment does not predict but prototypes: it makes futures available for collective examination before they arrive. Complements retcon (which releases the past's unrealized potential) with speculative gesture (which rehearses what might come).

**References:**
- LÜTTICKEN, Sven. "Performing Time." In *General Performance*. Berlin: Sternberg Press, 2013.
