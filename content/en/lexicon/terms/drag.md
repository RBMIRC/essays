Poliks and Trillo's term (*Exocapitalism*, 2025) for the attempt to capture resources from structures that escape human scale. Survival tactics employed in territories capital has abandoned: disability payments, platform gig work via smartphone, informal economy, family networks that redistribute what remains. *Drag* operates within *lift*'s aftermath, not opposing it but parasitizing its residue. The Eden project's workshops can be understood as *drag* gestures—using the means of *lift* (grant infrastructure, institutional mobility) to produce something that remains in the territory.

**References:**
- POLIKS, Marek, and Roberto Alonso TRILLO. *Exocapitalism: Economies with Absolutely No Limits*. Berlin/Nicosia: Becoming Press, 2025.
