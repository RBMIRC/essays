Steyerl's concept (2009) of degraded, compressed, copied images that circulate freely; low resolution as condition of accessibility and democratic distribution. Opposes the "rich image" of institutional custody—high resolution, controlled access, fetishized original. Poor images are "the debris of audiovisual production," uploaded, downloaded, shared, reformatted until they become "lumpen proletariat in the class society of appearances." Their degradation indexes their journey; their poverty is their democracy. Foundational for understanding how archival materials circulate in the Eden project.
