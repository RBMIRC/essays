Theoretical transposition of sonic practice to epistemology; extracting field of potential from existing materials, citation as material transformation. Sampling does not quote (represent) but lifts (incorporate and transform). The sample carries traces of its origin while becoming something new in recombination. AI training is massive sampling; inference is remix. Foundational for understanding statistical commons.

**References:**
- ESHUN, Kodwo. *More Brilliant than the Sun: Adventures in Sonic Fiction*. London: Quartet Books, 1998.
- NAVAS, Eduardo. *Remix Theory: The Aesthetics of Sampling*. Vienna: Springer, 2012.
