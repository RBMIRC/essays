Poliks and Trillo's concept (2025) describing capitalism operating as autonomous environment beyond human scale. Capital as computational pluriverse functioning through *lift* (extraction/departure), *drag* (parasitic survival), and *fold* (metamorphic recombination) rather than through embodied labor. Eden, NC exemplifies exocapitalist terrain: capital has not disappeared but operates at a distance—through financial markets that dismantled Pillowtex, through platform algorithms that make Eden reappear in rental yield circuits. The local appears disconnected precisely because connection operates in registers invisible at human scale.

**References:**
- POLIKS, Marek, and Roberto Alonso TRILLO. *Exocapitalism: Economies with Absolutely No Limits*. Berlin/Nicosia: Becoming Press, 2025.
