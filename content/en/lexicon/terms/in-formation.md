Simondon/Massumi's concept of the process of taking form; plane of mutual inclusion prior to stabilized structure. Emphasizes becoming over being: entities are not formed but in-formation, always in process. The hyphen marks the activity: not information (static content) but in-formation (dynamic shaping). Crucial for understanding how workshop outputs are never finished but caught in ongoing transformation.

**References:**
- SIMONDON, Gilbert. *Individuation in Light of Notions of Form and Information*. Trans. Taylor Adkins. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2020.
- MASSUMI, Brian. *Parables for the Virtual*. Durham: Duke University Press, 2002.
