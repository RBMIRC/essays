*Author's term* for imaginative extension grounded in archival evidence; speculation that amplifies rather than fabricates, maintaining accountability to sources while exploring unrealized possibilities. Distinguishes the Eden project's approach from pure fabulation: every speculative gesture can be traced to documentary origins, even when those origins are transformed beyond recognition. Sourced speculation operates between the "ça-a-été" (what was) and the "ça-aurait-pu-être" (what could have been).
