Simondon's concept of the technical environment created by and necessary for an object's functioning. The technical object and its milieu co-constitute each other through ongoing individuation. A combustion engine creates its associated milieu (cooling system, fuel delivery) while depending on it. The Eden project's associated milieu includes: grant infrastructure, international mobility, university affiliation, computational tools—all co-produced with the practice rather than pre-existing it.

**References:**
- SIMONDON, Gilbert. *On the Mode of Existence of Technical Objects*. Trans. Cécile Malaspina and John Rogove. Minneapolis: Univocal, 2017.
