*Author's term* for images produced from statistical distributions; neither present nor absent, haunting the archive without indexical anchor. The spectral image is not "fake" (implying a true original) but ghostly: it emerges from the statistical residue of countless images, bearing their traces without being any of them. Neither ça-a-été (that-has-been) nor pure invention, but spectral presence.
