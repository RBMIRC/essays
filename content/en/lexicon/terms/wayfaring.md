Ingold's concept of exploratory walk that engenders path by walking; knowledge through movement rather than representation. The wayfarer does not follow a pre-existing route (navigation) but generates the path through attentive movement (wayfaring). Knowledge accumulates not as map but as bodily skill. The Eden project as decade-long wayfaring: the practice generates its own trajectory through accumulated attentiveness.
