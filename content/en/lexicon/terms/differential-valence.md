*Author's term* for how the same technical process produces distinct ethical meanings depending on source materials' prior orientation. GPL-trained models differ ethically from proprietary-trained ones despite identical architecture: the statistical commons inherits the ethical valence of its sources. Differential valence makes ethics a question of provenance rather than intention—what the model was trained on matters more than what the operator intends.
