Massumi's concept of the germinal plane where potentials coexist before differentiating; relational field from which individuations emerge. Not a container but an active milieu: potentials "belong" to each other through their mutual implication before actualizing as distinct entities. The workshop creates a temporary field of belonging from which unexpected collaborations can emerge.

**References:**
- MASSUMI, Brian. *Semblance and Event: Activist Philosophy and the Occurrent Arts*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2011.
