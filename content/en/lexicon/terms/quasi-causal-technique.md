Massumi's concept of gesture creating conditions for event rather than mechanically producing it. Quasi-causal techniques enable rather than determine: they prepare the ground, increase likelihood, invite emergence—but do not guarantee outcomes. The workshop is quasi-causal: it creates conditions under which unexpected productions become possible without predetermining what will be produced.

**References:**
- MASSUMI, Brian. *Semblance and Event: Activist Philosophy and the Occurrent Arts*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2011.
