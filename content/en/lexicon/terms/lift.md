Poliks and Trillo's term (*Exocapitalism*, 2025) for capital's movement away from human affairs toward more efficient substrates. The tearing-away that leaves territories like Eden as residue. *Lift* proceeds through stages: offshore factory, logistics platform (Amazon replacing downtown retail), financialization (yield on Pillowtex stock rather than sheet production), recursive software economy where price decouples from material production. Physical things are slow, predictable, expensive—they impose fixed costs that constrain capital's movement. Eden is what *lift* leaves behind. The \$1,000 house indexes not singularity but the ordinariness of abandonment.

**References:**
- POLIKS, Marek, and Roberto Alonso TRILLO. *Exocapitalism: Economies with Absolutely No Limits*. Berlin/Nicosia: Becoming Press, 2025.
