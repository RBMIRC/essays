Historical practice of itinerant book peddling—colporteurs traveling with books and pamphlets to reach populations beyond urban centers. Crucial for disseminating Reformation texts, radical literature, and popular knowledge before mass literacy. Benjamin uses "colportage" in the Arcades Project to describe the circulation of images and ideas through commercial networks. Historical precedent for understanding how knowledge moves through informal channels, bypassing institutional gatekeepers.
