Italian theorist and activist (b. 1949), key figure of post-autonomia. *The Soul at Work* (2009) analyzes how cognitive capitalism captures the soul itself—creativity, affect, attention. "Semio-capitalism" names the regime where signs and semiotics become primary site of value extraction. *After the Future* (2011) on the exhaustion of future-oriented imagination. *Heroes* (2015) on mass murder and suicide as symptoms of semiotic saturation. Connects autonomist politics to mental health, exhaustion, and the crisis of attention under info-capitalism.

**Key Concepts:** Semio-capitalism, Cognitariat, Soul at Work, Exhaustion
