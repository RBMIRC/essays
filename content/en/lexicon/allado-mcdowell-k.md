Writer, artist, and musician; founder of the Artists + Machine Intelligence program at Google AI. Pioneer of human-AI literary collaboration. Their book *Pharmako-AI* (2020), co-written with GPT-3, explores consciousness, ecology, and technology through an unprecedented creative partnership with a language model. Subsequent works *Amor Cringe* (2022) and *Air Age Blueprint* (2022) continue this collaborative practice. Their work demonstrates that AI collaboration need not be extractive or replacement-oriented but can constitute a genuine creative dialogue—a model of transindividuation rather than automation. Challenges both techno-utopian and dystopian narratives by practicing what they theorize.

**Key Works:**
- ALLADO-MCDOWELL, K. *Pharmako-AI*. London: Ignota Books, 2020.
- ALLADO-MCDOWELL, K. *Amor Cringe*. London: Ignota Books, 2022.
