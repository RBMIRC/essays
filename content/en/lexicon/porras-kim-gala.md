Colombian-Korean American artist (b. 1984) whose practice investigates the politics of museum collection, conservation, and display. Works propose "speculative conservation"—imagining how objects might be treated if institutional protocols were altered. *Precipitation for an Arid Landscape* (2021) addresses the British Museum's contested holdings; other works examine linguistic extinction and the preservation of endangered knowledge. Demonstrates how artistic intervention can reveal the ideological assumptions embedded in archival and curatorial practice.
