*Author's term* for refraction of heterogeneous frameworks revealing frequencies none could render visible alone. Distinct from synthesis (which merges frameworks into unity) and eclecticism (which samples without transformation), the prismatic method separates and recombines. Like a prism dispersing white light into spectrum, the method reveals components that were always present but undifferentiated.
