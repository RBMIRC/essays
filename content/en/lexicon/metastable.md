Simondon's concept of a state of dynamic equilibrium containing unresolved tensions; system poised between stability and transformation, capable of individuation. Unlike stable equilibrium (which resists change) or unstable equilibrium (which collapses immediately), metastability maintains tension as resource. The pre-individual field is metastable: rich with potential, awaiting the trigger that precipitates individuation.

- SIMONDON, Gilbert. *Individuation in Light of Notions of Form and Information*. Trans. Taylor Adkins. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2020.
