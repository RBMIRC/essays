*Author's term* for tactic with duration and accumulated knowledge without becoming strategy. Retains tactical flexibility (responsive to situation) while building on prior iterations (learning across time). De Certeau's tactics are ephemeral; strategies require institutional stability. The sedimented tactic occupies the middle: continuity without fixity, memory without rigidity. The Eden project as decade-long sedimented tactic.

**References:**
- DE CERTEAU, Michel. *The Practice of Everyday Life*. Trans. Steven Rendall. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1984.
- Developed in "Retcon as Method" (this corpus).
