Optimization algorithm minimizing error by iteratively adjusting parameters in the direction that reduces loss. Computes gradient (slope) of error function, takes step in opposite direction. Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) uses random samples rather than full dataset, enabling efficient training on large corpora. The mechanism by which neural networks "learn": gradual adjustment toward configurations that produce less error. Combined with backpropagation to train deep networks.
