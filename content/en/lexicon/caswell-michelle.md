American archivist and scholar whose work theorizes community archives and their affective dimensions. Developed concept of "affective bonds"—the care relationships between archivists and communities that shape archival practice beyond institutional protocols. Co-founder of the South Asian American Digital Archive (SAADA). Work demonstrates how marginalized communities build archives that serve different purposes than institutional repositories: not neutral preservation but active care, not professional distance but affective investment.

**Key Concepts:** [[affective-bonds|Affective Bonds]], Community Archives, Representational Belonging
