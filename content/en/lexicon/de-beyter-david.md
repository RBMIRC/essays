French photographer (b. 1985, Roubaix). Trained at La Cambre photography department in Brussels (2008) and Le Fresnoy, Studio national des arts contemporains (2010). His approach, both conceptual and documentary, explores the boundaries between reality and fiction. His project *Big Bangers* (2015-2018) documents a Northern European community practicing car crashing as a way of life—the resulting wrecks being called "auto-sculptures" in amateur jargon. The work combines film, photography, and sculpture, interrogating collective rituals around automotive obsolescence. Resonates with Author's interest in reenactment as method and the structural melancholy of post-industrial landscapes.
