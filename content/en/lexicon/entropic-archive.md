*Author's term* for archives subject to dissolution and decay. Opposes the fantasy of perfect preservation while recognizing entropy as constitutive condition rather than threat. The entropic archive does not fight degradation but incorporates it as data—what disappears is as significant as what persists. Complements the *prismatic archive* (which diffracts) and the *speculative archive* (which projects forward).

**References:**
- STIEGLER, Bernard. *Qu'appelle-t-on panser?* 3 vols. Paris: Les Liens qui libèrent, 2018–2020.
