French curator and theorist. *Relational Aesthetics* (1998) theorized 1990s art practices (Tiravanija, Gillick, Parreno) as creating social situations rather than objects. Art becomes "a state of encounter" producing intersubjective relations. Later *Postproduction* (2002) analyzed artists working with existing cultural forms. *The Radicant* (2009) proposed "altermodernity" against both modernism and postmodernism. Contested by Bishop for privileging conviviality over antagonism; remains influential framework for understanding participatory and process-based practices.
