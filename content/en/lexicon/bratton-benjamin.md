American design theorist and philosopher. *The Stack: On Software and Sovereignty* (2015) theorizes planetary computation as geopolitical architecture organized in six layers (Earth, Cloud, City, Address, Interface, User). Later works including *The Revenge of the Real* (2021) address pandemic governance and planetary sensing. Argues for "positive biopolitics" and "synthetic rationality" against both technophobic rejection and naive techno-utopianism. Essential for understanding computation as spatial and political infrastructure rather than mere tool.

**Key Concepts:** [[stack-the|The Stack]], Planetary Computation, Synthetic Rationality
