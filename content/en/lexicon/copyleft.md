---

Licensing mechanism ensuring that derivatives of collective production remain collectively available. Uses copyright law against itself: you may use, modify, and distribute, but you must extend the same freedoms to others. Foundational for statistical commons: the preponderance of GPL-licensed code in AI training data creates what Author calls "differential valence"—ethical orientation embedded in the weights themselves.

**References:**
- STALLMAN, Richard. "Copyleft: Pragmatic Idealism." In *Free Software, Free Society*. Boston: GNU Press, 2002.
- KELTY, Christopher M. *Two Bits: The Cultural Significance of Free Software*. Durham: Duke University Press, 2008.
