Simondon's concept of the ontological domain prior to individuation; field of potentials from which individuals emerge while never exhausting it. The pre-individual is not chronologically prior (like an origin) but ontologically constitutive: it persists alongside and within the individuated, as reserve of potential for further transformation. The "collective" is not aggregate of individuals but access to shared pre-individual.

**References:**
- SIMONDON, Gilbert. *Individuation in Light of Notions of Form and Information*. Trans. Taylor Adkins. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2020.
