American architect, systems theorist, and inventor (1895-1983). At Black Mountain College, built the first geodesic dome (1948) with students, demonstrating that structural integrity emerges from network relations rather than massive elements. Developed *synergetics* and named *tensegrity* (with Snelson). Fuller's BMC work exemplifies learning through building: theory and practice simultaneous, failure as data. His geodesic structures anticipate network theory—strength through distribution, whole exceeding parts.
