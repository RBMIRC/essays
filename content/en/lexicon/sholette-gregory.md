American artist, writer, and activist. *Dark Matter* (2011) theorizes invisible artistic labor sustaining the art world. Co-founder of Political Art Documentation/Distribution (PAD/D, 1980-1988) and REPOhistory (1989-2000). Work investigates "bare art"—cultural production at margins of visibility. Argues that failed, amateur, and collective practices constitute majority of art world's mass while remaining unrecognized. Essential for understanding the political economy of artistic labor.

**Key Concepts:** [[dark-matter|Dark Matter]], Bare Art, Creative Dark Matter
