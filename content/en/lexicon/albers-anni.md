German-American textile artist and printmaker (1899-1994). At BMC (1933-1949) with husband Josef, developed weaving as rigorous artistic practice. *On Weaving* (1965) theorizes textile as proto-computational medium: warp and weft as binary system, pattern emerging from systematic variation. First textile artist given solo exhibition at MoMA (1949). Work demonstrates how material constraints generate rather than limit possibility. Relevant to Author's interest in proto-computational practices at BMC.

**Key Concepts:** Weaving as Computation, Material Practice, Haptic Intelligence
