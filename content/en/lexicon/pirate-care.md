Concept developed by Valeria Graziano, Marcell Mars, and Tomislav Medak describing care practices that must operate outside legal frameworks due to the criminalization of solidarity. When states fail to provide care—or actively prevent it—pirate care emerges: shadow libraries providing access to knowledge, mutual aid networks distributing resources, search-and-rescue operations in the Mediterranean. Not merely illegal but extra-legal: care that cannot wait for permission. Related to "custodianship" as practice of maintaining commons without institutional authorization.
