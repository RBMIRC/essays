French philosopher (1952-2020) who developed a theory of technical memory and its role in human individuation, crucial for understanding AI's transformation of collective memory. Key concepts include [[Tertiary Retention]], [[Tertiary Protention]], [[Proletarianization]], [[Pharmacological]], [[Grammatization]], and [[Transindividuation]].

**Key Works:** *Technics and Time* (1994-2001), *For a New Critique of Political Economy* (2009)
