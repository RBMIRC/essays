## A

- [Abduction](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/abduction) — Speculative reasoning from incomplete information
- [Adherent Referent](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/adherent-referent) — Barthes's concept of photography's physical link to its subject
- [Affective Bonds](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/affective-bonds) — Caswell's concept of care relationships in community archives
- [AI (Artificial Intelligence)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/ai-artificial-intelligence) — Scientific field aimed at creating systems capable of performing complex tasks. In the Heredoc Manifesto, it is a "pharmakon" (remedy and poison) and a political apparatus that structurally transforms intellectual property
- [Algorithmic Governmentality](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/algorithmic-governmentality) — Rouvroy and Berns's concept (2010-2013) analyzing new forms of power operating through statistical prediction and pre-emption
- [Algorithmic Hallucination](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/algorithmic-hallucination) — Transposition of clinical "hallucination" to computational systems; describes AI filling archival gaps through statistical continuity, producing plausible but unfounded outputs. Term circulating in AI discourse; Author's specific application to archival theory
- [Amplification (vs. Extraction)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/amplification-vs-extraction) — Author's term opposing extractivist logics (cf. Couldry/Mejias's Data Colonialism); directing attention toward sources rather than absorbing them into new outputs. Methodological counterpoint to appropriation
- [Apparatus (dispositif)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/apparatus-dispositif) — Foucault's concept of material-social arrangements
- [Archival Phantasm](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/archival-phantasm) — Author's term combining Derrida's archive (Archive Fever) with psychoanalytic phantasm; the spectral image produced from statistical distributions, neither present nor absent
- [Artificial Neural Network](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/artificial-neural-network) — Computational architecture composed of layers of interconnected "neurons." Knowledge compresses in the weights of these connections

## B

- [Bad Infinity](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/bad-infinity) — Hegel's concept of degenerate endless repetition
- [Behavioral Surplus](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/behavioral-surplus) — Zuboff's term for data extracted beyond service needs
- [Bifurcation (Capability of)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/bifurcation-capability-of) — Author's pedagogical extension of Stiegler's concept (Bifurquer, 2020); where Stiegler theorizes systemic bifurcation against entropy, Author emphasizes individual/collective capacity to change direction radically within workshop conditions
- [Black Box](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/black-box) — Latour's term for hidden internal complexity
- [Black Mountain College (BMC)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/black-mountain-college-bmc) — Experimental college (1933-1957) as methodological operator

## C

- [Ça-a-été (That-has-been)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/a-a-t-that-has-been) — Barthes's term for photography's testimony of presence
- [Capabilities](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/capabilities) — Sen/Nussbaum's concept of effective possibilities for action
- [Capitalist Realism](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/capitalist-realism) — Fisher's term for the impossibility of imagining alternatives
- [Ch'ixi](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/ch-ixi) — Rivera Cusicanqui's Aymara concept of productive contradiction
- [Chronopolitics](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/chronopolitics) — The politics of time as instrument of power; term dating from 1970 (Social Forces), developed through Maier and Clark's historiography, and reappropriated by Rasheedah Phillips/Black Quantum Futurism (2016) to theorize "temporal ghettos" of racial capitalism and Afrofuturist counter-temporalities
- [Circulationism](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/circulationism) — Steyerl's term (2014) replacing Soviet productivism; value created not through making images but through post-producing, launching, and accelerating them across networks. Marks shift from production to circulation as site of meaning
- [Cosmotechnics](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/cosmotechnics) — Hui's concept of technology as cultural-cosmic articulation
- [Contracted Time](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/contracted-time) — Author's term opposing "expanded time" of contemplation; intensive temporality of the workshop where compression generates pedagogical friction rather than reflection

## D

- [Data Colonialism](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/data-colonialism) — Couldry/Mejias's concept of data as new form of colonial extraction
- [Dataset / Training Corpus](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/dataset-training-corpus) — Massive set of data used to train models. The preponderance of open source code (~60%) imbues models with a "tropism toward sharing"
- [Deep Learning](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/deep-learning) — Machine learning through layered neural networks; depth makes extracting data provenance mathematically intractable
- [Deproletarianization](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/deproletarianization) — Stiegler's concept of recovering lost knowledge capacities
- [Diffraction (Method)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/diffraction-method) — Haraway's alternative to reflection for thinking difference
- [Distributed Cognition](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/distributed-cognition) — Hutchins's concept of thinking across human-technical systems

## E

- [Ecology of Friction](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/ecology-of-friction) — Author's term inverting "frictionless" design ideology (cf. platform capitalism's seamlessness); resistance as pedagogical resource rather than obstacle to overcome
- [Embodied Knowledge](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/embodied-knowledge) — Knowledge held in the body through practice
- [Embedding](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/embedding) — Dense vector representation of an element in latent space; where "license ghosts" persist as spectral traces, now mathematically inextricable
- [Epiphylogenesis](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/epiphylogenesis) — Stiegler's concept of evolution through technical memory
- [Epistemic Extractivism](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/epistemic-extractivism) — Rivera Cusicanqui's concept of knowledge appropriation
- [Exposed Teacher](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/exposed-teacher) — Author's term opposing the "master" who demonstrates finished competence; pedagogue who shows process, uncertainty, and vulnerability rather than mastery. Resonates with Rancière's ignorant schoolmaster but emphasizes visibility of struggle

## F

- [Fork](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/fork) — Copying a project entirely to create an independent version; maintains genealogical link with original while allowing autonomous evolution (a plant "cutting")
- [Free Labor](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/free-labor) — Terranova's concept of unpaid digital work
- [Friction (Pedagogical)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/friction-pedagogical) — *Author's term*: productive resistance in learning

## G

- [GAN (Generative Adversarial Network)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/gan-generative-adversarial-network) — Neural architecture for image generation
- [Gestural Community](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/gestural-community) — Author's term extending Agamben's gesture (Means Without Ends) to collective practice; community formed through shared physical actions rather than shared beliefs or identities
- [Grammatization](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/grammatization) — Stiegler's concept of discretizing continuous flows

## H

- [Hauntology](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/hauntology) — Derrida/Fisher's concept of spectral persistence
- [Heredoc](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/heredoc) — Unix syntax as metaphor for collective authorship

## I

- [Image Continuum](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/image-continuum) — Author's term for the ontological spectrum from indexical photography (Barthes's ça-a-été) to statistical generation (Steyerl's Mean Images); maps the transition rather than opposing the two poles
- [Incomputability](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/incomputability) — Chaitin's concept of irreducible complexity
- [Indexical Dispensation](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/indexical-dispensation) — Author's term for the historical regime of photography-as-evidence; the epistemological contract (now dissolving) that images testify to what was physically present before the lens
- [Individuation](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/individuation) — Simondon's concept of becoming through process
- [Inference](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/inference) — Phase where models generate new data in response to a prompt; the probabilistic process through which AI "manufactures public domain"
- [Inflection](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/inflection) — Deleuze's concept of intensive change within continuity

## J

- [Joint Attention](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/joint-attention) — Shared focus as condition for collective learning

## K

- [Kaitiakitanga](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/kaitiakitanga) — Māori concept of responsible guardianship
- [Know-How (vs. Know-That)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/know-how-vs-know-that) — Practical vs. propositional knowledge

## L

- [Latent Space](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/latent-space) — Abstract mathematical space where data is represented in compressed form; "a probabilistic ocean where all solutions ever written float in a state of pure virtuality"
- [Liquid Archive](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/liquid-archive) — Author's theoretical concept (distinct from artistic projects using this title, e.g., MIT Museum 2022); archive that generates rather than stores, extending Bauman's liquid modernity to archival practice
- [Liquid Modernity](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/liquid-modernity) — Bauman's concept of fluid contemporary social forms
- [LLM (Large Language Model)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/llm-large-language-model) — Very large language model trained on billions of words and code; the main agents of the transmutation of private code into statistical commons
- [Lost Futures](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/lost-futures) — Fisher's concept of unrealized historical possibilities
- [Lumbung](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/lumbung) — Indonesian concept of collective resource sharing

## M

- [Machine Learning](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/machine-learning) — AI subdomain where systems learn from data (patterns) rather than explicit programming; the paradigm that makes the dissolution of attribution possible
- [Maintainers](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/maintainers) — Guardians of original projects who decide which contributions to accept or reject; representing the central authority contested by the Heredoc Manifesto
- [Mean Image](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/mean-image) — Steyerl's term (2023) playing on "mean" as both mathematical average and cruelty; statistical composites in AI-generated images that erase singularity. Shows how generative models flatten difference into normative representations
- [Model Collapse](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/model-collapse) — Shumailov et al.'s term (Nature, 2024) for degenerative process when generative models train recursively on synthetic data; causes irreversible loss of distributional tails and minority information. Also called "Habsburg AI" or "AI inbreeding"
- [Monotechnics](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/monotechnics) — Single technological paradigm imposed as universal

## N

- [Negentropic Obligation](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/negentropic-obligation) — Author's ethical term extending Stiegler's negentropy (Neganthropocene); where Stiegler theorizes negentropy as life's resistance to disorder, Author formulates the duty to maintain difference and singularity against homogenizing entropy
- [New Aesthetic](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/new-aesthetic) — Bridle's concept (SXSW 2012) describing the eruption of the digital into the physical world: glitches, pixelation, surveillance imagery becoming visible in everyday life

## O

- [Opacity](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/opacity) — Glissant's concept of the right not to be understood
- [Optical Unconscious](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/optical-unconscious) — Benjamin's concept of what photography reveals beyond intention

## P

- [Parameter / Weight](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/parameter-weight) — Adjustable floating-point number constituting neural network "memory"; encodes complex statistical correlations, the physical materialization of statistical commons
- [Pharmacological](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/pharmacological) — Stiegler's concept of technology as both poison and cure
- [Pluriverse](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/pluriverse) — Multiple coexisting cosmologies vs. single universe
- [Post-Indexical Image](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/post-indexical-image) — Author's term for images without indexical structure; neither "fake" (implying a true original) nor "synthetic" (implying artificiality), but operating in a different ontological register altogether
- [Prismatic Archive](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/prismatic-archive) — Author's term opposing both "dissolved" archive (lost to entropy) and "frozen" archive (fixed in time); archive that diffracts rather than dissolves, producing spectra rather than unities
- [Proletarianization (of the Mind)](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/proletarianization-of-the-mind) — Stiegler's concept of knowledge loss through automation
- [Prompt Engineering](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/prompt-engineering) — The art of formulating optimal instructions to obtain results from an LLM; the prompt as "new literacy" and "incantation" addressed to compressed collective intelligence
- [Pull Request](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/pull-request) — Modification proposal in software development: "Here are the improvements I made in my fork, would you like to integrate them into your version?"

## R

- [Reactivation](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/reactivation) — Making latent archival possibilities available for present use
- [Recursive Archive](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/recursive-archive) — Author's term for archive that queries itself; where traditional archives await external interrogation, recursive archives generate questions about their own conditions and gaps
- [Repository](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/repository) — Digital space where a software project lives: a library containing all files, the history of modifications, and the project's rules
- [Retcon](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/retcon) — Retroactive continuity as creative-critical method
- [Rhizome](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/rhizome) — Deleuze/Guattari's concept (A Thousand Plateaus) materialized through technical architecture: structure without fixed center or hierarchy, where knowledge circulates between equal peers

## S

- [Situated Knowledge](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/situated-knowledge) — Haraway's concept of knowledge from embodied position
- [Statistical Commons](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/statistical-commons) — Author's term for shared probability distributions produced by AI training; commons created not through intentional sharing but through mechanical aggregation of human expression into collective statistical patterns
- [Statistical Unconscious](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/statistical-unconscious) — Schröter's term (2023) transposing Benjamin's Optical Unconscious to AI image generation; what machine learning reveals in aggregate patterns that escapes conscious human perception. Where Benjamin's camera revealed hidden gestures, AI reveals hidden distributions
- [Statistics](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/statistics) — Sciences of data analysis and interpretation (etym. "sciences of the State"); in AI context, emphasizes that knowledge emerges through sedimentation and probabilistic recombination
- [Surveillance Capitalism](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/surveillance-capitalism) — Zuboff's concept of economy based on behavioral prediction
- [Symbiotic Algorithm](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/symbiotic-algorithm) — Author's term opposing extractive computation; algorithm as relation rather than extraction, mutual transformation of human and machine

## T

- [Tacit Knowledge](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/tacit-knowledge) — Polanyi's concept of knowledge that cannot be fully articulated
- [Tactical Urgency](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/tactical-urgency) — Author's term combining de Certeau's tactics (The Practice of Everyday Life) with temporal pressure; time constraint as strategic resource rather than limitation, generating invention through compression
- [Technical Culture](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/technical-culture) — Simondon's concept of literacy about technical objects
- [Technodiversity](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/technodiversity) — Hui's concept of multiple technical traditions
- [Tertiary Retention](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/tertiary-retention) — Stiegler's concept of externalized technical memory
- [Tertiary Protention](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/tertiary-protention) — Hui's concept (2016) supplementing Stiegler's tertiary retention; where Stiegler theorized externalized memory (retention), Hui adds externalized anticipation—technologies that structure futurity, from debt to algorithmic prediction
- [Token / Tokenization](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/token-tokenization) — Process of fragmenting text or code into elementary units that algorithms manipulate mathematically; the first step in dissolving semantic integrity
- [Training](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/training) — Process by which models learn by iteratively adjusting billions of weights to minimize error; emphasizes the massive ecological cost of this knowledge transformation
- [Transduction](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/transduction) — Simondon's concept of propagating transformation
- [Transformer](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/transformer) — Neural network architecture (2017) that made modern LLMs possible through the attention mechanism; accelerating compression and recombination
- [Transindividuation](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/transindividuation) — Stiegler's concept of individual-collective co-constitution

## U

- [Unboxing](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/unboxing) — Author's theoretical term (distinct from consumer "unboxing" videos); opening Latour's black box to reveal internal complexity, but emphasizing the pedagogical act of exposure rather than mere technical analysis
- [Universe of Technical Images](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/universe-of-technical-images) — Flusser's concept of autonomous image realm

## V

- [Vertical Work](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/vertical-work) — Author's term opposing "horizontal" connection (networking, linking, sharing); deep engagement with singular problems rather than lateral movement across surfaces. Resonates with Stiegler's critique of horizontal proletarianization
- [Vibe Coding](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/vibe-coding) — Karpathy's term (February 2025) for AI-assisted programming where developers "forget that the code even exists"; extends his 2023 claim that "the hottest new programming language is English." Marks shift from writing to prompting
- [Visible Labor](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/visible-labor) — Author's inversion of "invisible labor" (Daniels 1987, Terranova's Free Labor); showing process rather than finished results, making pedagogical and creative work apparent rather than concealed

## W

- [Whakapapa](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/whakapapa) — Māori concept of genealogical interconnection
- [Workshop](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/workshop) — *Author's term*: pedagogical dispositif of intensive collective making

## Y

- [Yellowing](/en/lexicon//en/lexicon/terms/yellowing) — Author's metaphor for model collapse, drawing on documented phenomenon of yellow/golden bias in AI-generated images (DALL-E, Midjourney, ChatGPT, 2025); visual symptom of distributional degradation where recursive training produces chromatic drift toward statistical means
