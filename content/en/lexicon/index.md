## A

- [Abduction](/en/lexicon/abduction) — Speculative reasoning from incomplete information
- [Albers, Anni](/en/lexicon/albers-anni) — German-American textile artist at BMC; weaving as proto-computation
- [Albers, Josef](/en/lexicon/albers-josef) — German-American artist and educator at BMC; developed Werklehre
- [Adherent Referent](/en/lexicon/adherent-referent) — Barthes's concept of photography's physical link to its subject
- [Affective Bonds](/en/lexicon/affective-bonds) — Caswell's concept of care relationships in community archives
- [AI (Artificial Intelligence)](/en/lexicon/ai-artificial-intelligence) — Scientific field aimed at creating systems capable of performing complex tasks. In the Heredoc Manifesto, it is a "pharmakon" (remedy and poison) and a political apparatus that structurally transforms intellectual property
- [Allado-McDowell, K](/en/lexicon/allado-mcdowell-k) — Writer and artist; Pharmako-AI and human-AI collaboration
- [API (Application Programming Interface)](/en/lexicon/api) — Standardized interface for software communication; mediates access to AI capabilities
- [Attention Mechanism](/en/lexicon/attention-mechanism) — Core Transformer innovation enabling selective focus across sequences
- [Autoencoder](/en/lexicon/autoencoder) — Neural network learning compressed representations through reconstruction
- [Algorithmic Governmentality](/en/lexicon/algorithmic-governmentality) — Rouvroy and Berns's concept (2010-2013) analyzing new forms of power operating through statistical prediction and pre-emption
- [Algorithmic Hallucination](/en/lexicon/algorithmic-hallucination) — Transposition of clinical "hallucination" to computational systems; describes AI filling archival gaps through statistical continuity, producing plausible but unfounded outputs. Term circulating in AI discourse; Author's specific application to archival theory
- [Amplification (vs. Extraction)](/en/lexicon/amplification-vs-extraction) — Author's term opposing extractivist logics (cf. Couldry/Mejias's Data Colonialism); directing attention toward sources rather than absorbing them into new outputs. Methodological counterpoint to appropriation
- [(An)archive](/en/lexicon/anarchive) — Wielocha's concept of open-ended rhizomatic structure where artwork identity is distributed across activations; neither fixed object nor pure event but ongoing process of becoming
- [Apparatus (dispositif)](/en/lexicon/apparatus-dispositif) — Foucault's concept of material-social arrangements
- [Archival Phantasm](/en/lexicon/archival-phantasm) — Author's term combining Derrida's archive (Archive Fever) with psychoanalytic phantasm; the spectral image produced from statistical distributions, neither present nor absent
- [Artificial Neural Network](/en/lexicon/artificial-neural-network) — Computational architecture composed of layers of interconnected "neurons." Knowledge compresses in the weights of these connections
- [Associated Milieu](/en/lexicon/associated-milieu) — Simondon's concept of the technical environment created by and necessary for an object's functioning; the technical object and its milieu co-constitute each other through ongoing individuation
- [Atlas Group](/en/lexicon/atlas-group) — Walid Raad's fictional archive project (1989-2004) on Lebanese civil wars
- [Archival Grain](/en/lexicon/archival-grain) — Stoler's method of reading colonial archives along their internal logic
- [Azoulay, Ariella Aïsha](/en/lexicon/azoulay-ariella) — Israeli-American theorist; potential history and unlearning imperialism

## B

- [Bad Infinity](/en/lexicon/bad-infinity) — Hegel's concept of degenerate endless repetition
- [Barthes, Roland](/en/lexicon/barthes-roland) — French literary theorist (1915-1980); Camera Lucida and photography theory
- [Baudrillard, Jean](/en/lexicon/baudrillard-jean) — French theorist; simulation, hyperreality, and precession of simulacra
- [Benjamin, Walter](/en/lexicon/benjamin-walter) — German philosopher (1892-1940); optical unconscious and mechanical reproduction
- [Behavioral Surplus](/en/lexicon/behavioral-surplus) — Zuboff's term for data extracted beyond service needs
- [Berardi, Franco (Bifo)](/en/lexicon/berardi-franco) — Italian theorist; semio-capitalism and cognitariat
- [Beings of Relation](/en/lexicon/beings-of-relation) — Glissant's concept of identity as constituted through encounter rather than essence; relational ontology where subjects emerge through contact with others
- [Bifurcation (Capability of)](/en/lexicon/bifurcation-capability-of) — Author's pedagogical extension of Stiegler's concept (Bifurquer, 2020); where Stiegler theorizes systemic bifurcation against entropy, Author emphasizes individual/collective capacity to change direction radically within workshop conditions
- [Black Box](/en/lexicon/black-box) — Latour's term for hidden internal complexity
- [Black Mountain College (BMC)](/en/lexicon/black-mountain-college-bmc) — Experimental college (1933-1957) as methodological operator
- [Backpropagation](/en/lexicon/backpropagation) — Algorithm propagating error backward through neural networks for training
- [Beautiful Risk of Education](/en/lexicon/beautiful-risk-of-education) — Biesta's concept defending education's inherent uncertainty
- [Biesta, Gert](/en/lexicon/biesta-gert) — Dutch educational theorist; critique of learnification
- [Bishop, Claire](/en/lexicon/bishop-claire) — British art historian; participatory art and antagonism
- [Bourriaud, Nicolas](/en/lexicon/bourriaud-nicolas) — French curator; relational aesthetics
- [Barad, Karen](/en/lexicon/barad-karen) — American theorist; agential realism and intra-action
- [Bennett, Jane](/en/lexicon/bennett-jane) — American theorist; vibrant matter and vital materialism
- [Bratton, Benjamin](/en/lexicon/bratton-benjamin) — American theorist; The Stack and planetary computation
- [Brathwaite-Shirley, Danielle](/en/lexicon/brathwaite-shirley-danielle) — British-Nigerian artist; digital archives of Black trans experience

## C

- [Cage, John](/en/lexicon/cage-john) — American composer at BMC; chance operations and indeterminacy
- [Ça-a-été (That-has-been)](/en/lexicon/a-a-t-that-has-been) — Barthes's term for photography's testimony of presence
- [Community of Practice](/en/lexicon/community-of-practice) — Lave/Wenger's term for social learning through participation
- [Cunningham, Merce](/en/lexicon/cunningham-merce) — American choreographer at BMC; chance-based dance
- [Capabilities](/en/lexicon/capabilities) — Sen/Nussbaum's concept of effective possibilities for action
- [Capitalist Realism](/en/lexicon/capitalist-realism) — Fisher's term for the impossibility of imagining alternatives
- [Ch'ixi](/en/lexicon/ch-ixi) — Rivera Cusicanqui's Aymara concept of productive contradiction
- [Chaosmosis](/en/lexicon/chaosmosis) — Guattari's concept of oscillation between chaos and emergence of provisional cosmos; creative process navigating between complete disorder and over-determined structure
- [Chronopolitics](/en/lexicon/chronopolitics) — The politics of time as instrument of power; term dating from 1970 (Social Forces), developed through Maier and Clark's historiography, and reappropriated by Rasheedah Phillips/Black Quantum Futurism (2016) to theorize "temporal ghettos" of racial capitalism and Afrofuturist counter-temporalities
- [Circulationism](/en/lexicon/circulationism) — Steyerl's term (2014) replacing Soviet productivism; value created not through making images but through post-producing, launching, and accelerating them across networks. Marks shift from production to circulation as site of meaning
- [Cosmotechnics](/en/lexicon/cosmotechnics) — Hui's concept of technology as cultural-cosmic articulation
- [Conceptechnics](/en/lexicon/conceptechnics) — Eshun's fusion of concept and sonic technique; sampling as mode of thought where citation becomes material transformation rather than mere reference
- [Contracted Time](/en/lexicon/contracted-time) — Author's term opposing "expanded time" of contemplation; intensive temporality of the workshop where compression generates pedagogical friction rather than reflection
- [Copyleft](/en/lexicon/copyleft) — Licensing mechanism ensuring that derivatives of collective production remain collectively available; uses copyright law against itself to prevent enclosure
- [Critical Fabulation](/en/lexicon/critical-fabulation) — Hartman's method of narrating lives absent from archives; speculation grounded in historical record that imagines possibilities the archive cannot confirm but also cannot exclude
- [Caswell, Michelle](/en/lexicon/caswell-michelle) — American archivist; affective bonds and community archives
- [Colportage](/en/lexicon/colportage) — Historical practice of itinerant book distribution
- [Continuous Prototype](/en/lexicon/continuous-prototype) — Design concept of work remaining permanently in prototype stage
- [Custodianship](/en/lexicon/custodianship) — Mars/Medak's term for maintaining knowledge commons without authorization
- [Citton, Yves](/en/lexicon/citton-yves) — French theorist; ecology of attention
- [Chto Delat](/en/lexicon/chto-delat) — Russian collective; art, theory, and activism
- [Cloud Computing](/en/lexicon/cloud-computing) — Remote computing resources; material infrastructure obscured by immaterial metaphor
- [Context Window](/en/lexicon/context-window) — Maximum text a language model processes at once; determines conversational memory

## D

- [Data Colonialism](/en/lexicon/data-colonialism) — Couldry/Mejias's concept of data as new form of colonial extraction
- [De Beyter, David](/en/lexicon/de-beyter-david) — Belgian artist; post-industrial landscapes and collective memory
- [Deller, Jeremy](/en/lexicon/deller-jeremy) — British artist; reenactment and collective participation
- [Derrida, Jacques](/en/lexicon/derrida-jacques) — French philosopher (1930-2004); archive fever and deconstruction
- [Dataset / Training Corpus](/en/lexicon/dataset-training-corpus) — Massive set of data used to train models. The preponderance of open source code (~60%) imbues models with a "tropism toward sharing"
- [Deep Learning](/en/lexicon/deep-learning) — Machine learning through layered neural networks; depth makes extracting data provenance mathematically intractable
- [Deproletarianization](/en/lexicon/deproletarianization) — Stiegler's concept of recovering lost knowledge capacities
- [Diffraction (Method)](/en/lexicon/diffraction-method) — Haraway's alternative to reflection for thinking difference
- [Diffusion Model](/en/lexicon/diffusion-model) — Generative model learning to reverse gradual noising; DALL-E, Stable Diffusion
- [Differential Valence](/en/lexicon/differential-valence) — Author's term for how the same technical process produces distinct ethical meanings depending on source materials' prior orientation; GPL-trained models differ ethically from proprietary-trained ones despite identical architecture
- [Distributed Cognition](/en/lexicon/distributed-cognition) — Hutchins's concept of thinking across human-technical systems
- [Double Becoming](/en/lexicon/double-becoming) — Deleuze/Guattari's concept of mutual alteration where both terms transform without one becoming the other; wasp and orchid each become something new through encounter
- [Drag](/en/lexicon/drag) — Poliks/Trillo's term (Exocapitalism, 2025) for the attempt to capture resources from structures that escape human scale; survival tactics in territories capital has abandoned (disability payments, platform gig work, informal economy)
- [Dark Matter](/en/lexicon/dark-matter) — Sholette's concept for invisible artistic labor sustaining art world
- [DeLanda, Manuel](/en/lexicon/delanda-manuel) — Mexican-American philosopher; assemblage theory
- [Didi-Huberman, Georges](/en/lexicon/didi-huberman-georges) — French art historian; surviving image and Nachleben
- [Duberman, Martin](/en/lexicon/duberman-martin) — American historian; definitive BMC history

## E

- [Ecology of Friction](/en/lexicon/ecology-of-friction) — Author's term inverting "frictionless" design ideology (cf. platform capitalism's seamlessness); resistance as pedagogical resource rather than obstacle to overcome
- [Education of Attention](/en/lexicon/education-of-attention) — Ingold's concept of learning to perceive what materials tell us through directed attention
- [Embodied Knowledge](/en/lexicon/embodied-knowledge) — Knowledge held in the body through practice
- [Embedding](/en/lexicon/embedding) — Dense vector representation of an element in latent space; where "license ghosts" persist as spectral traces, now mathematically inextricable
- [Epiphylogenesis](/en/lexicon/epiphylogenesis) — Stiegler's concept of evolution through technical memory
- [Elasticity of the Almost](/en/lexicon/elasticity-of-the-almost) — Manning's concept of microperception sensing possible steps before they occur; the felt potential of movement not yet taken but already shaping present action
- [Epistemic Extractivism](/en/lexicon/epistemic-extractivism) — Rivera Cusicanqui's concept of knowledge appropriation
- [Entropic Archive](/en/lexicon/entropic-archive) — Author's term for archives subject to dissolution and decay; opposes the fantasy of perfect preservation while recognizing entropy as constitutive condition rather than threat
- [Ethopoiesis](/en/lexicon/ethopoiesis) — Despret's concept (via Massumi) of self-inventive becoming of forms of life in relation; how organisms create new modes of existence through encounter
- [Exocapitalism](/en/lexicon/exocapitalism) — Poliks/Trillo's concept (2025) describing capitalism operating as autonomous environment beyond human scale; capital as computational pluriverse functioning through lift, drag, and fold rather than through embodied labor
- [Eshun, Kodwo](/en/lexicon/eshun-kodwo) — British theorist; Afrofuturism and sonic theory
- [Expanded Field](/en/lexicon/expanded-field) — Krauss's concept mapping sculpture's expansion beyond traditional definition
- [Exposed Teacher](/en/lexicon/exposed-teacher)
- [Extensive Continuum](/en/lexicon/extensive-continuum) — Whitehead's concept of the relational field underlying all actual occasions; potentiality for connection prior to any particular actualization — Author's term opposing the "master" who demonstrates finished competence; pedagogue who shows process, uncertainty, and vulnerability rather than mastery. Resonates with Rancière's ignorant schoolmaster but emphasizes visibility of struggle

## F

- [Fallibilism](/en/lexicon/fallibilism) — Pragmatist principle that all knowledge claims are provisional and subject to revision; Peirce's foundational insight that certainty is unattainable but inquiry can still progress. What survives critique of meliorism: experimentation without guarantee
- [Field of Belonging](/en/lexicon/field-of-belonging)
- [Fisher, Mark](/en/lexicon/fisher-mark) — British cultural theorist (1968-2017); capitalist realism and hauntology
- [Flusser, Vilém](/en/lexicon/flusser-vilem) — Czech-Brazilian philosopher (1920-1991); technical images and apparatus theory
- [Foucault, Michel](/en/lexicon/foucault-michel) — French philosopher (1926-1984); archive, dispositif, and power/knowledge — Massumi's concept of the germinal plane where potentials coexist before differentiating; relational field from which individuations emerge
- [Fork](/en/lexicon/fork) — Copying a project entirely to create an independent version; maintains genealogical link with original while allowing autonomous evolution (a plant "cutting")
- [Free Labor](/en/lexicon/free-labor) — Terranova's concept of unpaid digital work
- [Fold](/en/lexicon/fold) — Poliks/Trillo's term (Exocapitalism, 2025) for computational operations that transform material into new configurations; neither lift (extraction) nor drag (resistance) but metamorphic recombination
- [Fine-tuning](/en/lexicon/fine-tuning) — Adapting pre-trained models to specific tasks through additional training
- [Foundation Model](/en/lexicon/foundation-model) — Large model trained on broad data, adaptable to many downstream tasks
- [Friction (Pedagogical)](/en/lexicon/friction-pedagogical) — *Author's term*: productive resistance in learning
- [Fuller, R. Buckminster](/en/lexicon/fuller-buckminster) — American architect at BMC; geodesic domes and synergetics
- [Freire, Paulo](/en/lexicon/freire-paulo) — Brazilian educator; pedagogy of the oppressed

## G

- [Gestural Community](/en/lexicon/gestural-community)
- [Glissant, Édouard](/en/lexicon/glissant-edouard) — Martinican philosopher (1928-2011); opacity and poetics of relation — Author's term extending Agamben's gesture (Means Without Ends) to collective practice; community formed through shared physical actions rather than shared beliefs or identities
- [Grammatization](/en/lexicon/grammatization) — Stiegler's concept of discretizing continuous flows
- [Gates, Theaster](/en/lexicon/gates-theaster) — American artist; urban regeneration and social practice
- [Gaignard, Geneviève](/en/lexicon/gaignard-genevieve) — American artist; photography, identity, and the performativity of race
- [Gramsci Monument](/en/lexicon/gramsci-monument) — Hirschhorn's participatory project at Forest Houses, Bronx (2013)
- [Gielen, Pascal](/en/lexicon/gielen-pascal) — Belgian sociologist; art and commonism
- [GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)](/en/lexicon/gpu) — Processor for parallel computation; material substrate of AI training
- [Gradient Descent](/en/lexicon/gradient-descent) — Optimization algorithm minimizing error by iterative parameter adjustment

## I

- [In-formation](/en/lexicon/in-formation) — Simondon/Massumi's concept of the process of taking form; plane of mutual inclusion prior to stabilized structure. Emphasizes becoming over being
- [Image Continuum](/en/lexicon/image-continuum) — Author's term for the ontological spectrum from indexical photography (Barthes's ça-a-été) to statistical generation (Steyerl's Mean Images); maps the transition rather than opposing the two poles
- [Incomputability](/en/lexicon/incomputability) — Chaitin's concept of irreducible complexity
- [Indexical Dispensation](/en/lexicon/indexical-dispensation) — Author's term for the historical regime of photography-as-evidence; the epistemological contract (now dissolving) that images testify to what was physically present before the lens
- [Individuation](/en/lexicon/individuation) — Simondon's concept of becoming through process
- [Inference](/en/lexicon/inference) — Phase where models generate new data in response to a prompt; the probabilistic process through which AI "manufactures public domain"
- [Inflection](/en/lexicon/inflection) — Deleuze's concept of intensive change within continuity
- [Intercessor](/en/lexicon/intercessor) — Deleuze's concept of the catalyst that creates conditions for mutual becoming; not mediator (standing between) but intercessor (intervening to transform both parties)
- [Ignorant Schoolmaster](/en/lexicon/ignorant-schoolmaster) — Rancière's concept (via Jacotot) of teaching without transmitting knowledge
- [Ingold, Tim](/en/lexicon/ingold-tim) — British anthropologist; education of attention and wayfaring
- [Illich, Ivan](/en/lexicon/illich-ivan) — Austrian philosopher; convivial tools and deschooling
- [Interval, The](/en/lexicon/interval-the) — Manning's concept for zone where relational movement occurs
- [Invisible Committee](/en/lexicon/invisible-committee) — Anonymous French collective; insurrection theory

- [Joint Attention](/en/lexicon/joint-attention) — Shared focus as condition for collective learning

- [Knotted Thinking](/en/lexicon/knotted-thinking) — Author's term for architectural cognition transformed by digital diagramming; thinking that proceeds through nodal connections rather than linear argument

## L

- [Lab School](/en/lexicon/lab-school) — Dewey's experimental school at University of Chicago (1896-1904); laboratory where pedagogical hypotheses were tested through inquiry, manual labor, and problem-solving rather than lecture. Direct precursor to BMC's educational philosophy
- [Laclau, Ernesto](/en/lexicon/laclau-ernesto) — Argentine theorist; post-Marxism and populism theory
- [Land, Nick](/en/lexicon/land-nick) — British philosopher; accelerationism and dark enlightenment
- [Latent Space](/en/lexicon/latent-space) — Abstract mathematical space where data is represented in compressed form; "a probabilistic ocean where all solutions ever written float in a state of pure virtuality"
- [Latour, Bruno](/en/lexicon/latour-bruno) — French sociologist (1947-2022); actor-network theory and black box
- [Lyotard, Jean-François](/en/lexicon/lyotard-jean-francois) — French philosopher; postmodern condition and incredulity toward metanarratives
- [Liquid Archive](/en/lexicon/liquid-archive) — Author's theoretical concept (distinct from artistic projects using this title, e.g., MIT Museum 2022); archive that generates rather than stores, extending Bauman's liquid modernity to archival practice
- [Liquid Modernity](/en/lexicon/liquid-modernity) — Bauman's concept of fluid contemporary social forms
- [LLM (Large Language Model)](/en/lexicon/llm-large-language-model) — Very large language model trained on billions of words and code; the main agents of the transmutation of private code into statistical commons
- [Lost Futures](/en/lexicon/lost-futures) — Fisher's concept of unrealized historical possibilities
- [Lift](/en/lexicon/lift) — Poliks/Trillo's term (Exocapitalism, 2025) for capital's movement away from human affairs toward more efficient substrates; the tearing-away that leaves territories like Eden as residue
- [Live Creature](/en/lexicon/live-creature) — Dewey's concept (Art as Experience, 1934) of organism in active transaction with environment; biological grounding of aesthetic experience through rhythm of doing and undergoing
- [Lumbung](/en/lexicon/lumbung) — Indonesian concept of collective resource sharing
- [Legitimate Peripheral Participation](/en/lexicon/legitimate-peripheral-participation) — Lave/Wenger's concept of learning through marginal involvement in communities of practice
- [Lave, Jean](/en/lexicon/lave-jean) — American anthropologist; situated learning theory

## M

- [Machine Learning](/en/lexicon/machine-learning) — AI subdomain where systems learn from data (patterns) rather than explicit programming; the paradigm that makes the dissolution of attribution possible
- [Mouffe, Chantal](/en/lexicon/mouffe-chantal) — Belgian theorist; agonistic pluralism and radical democracy
- [Machinic Phylum](/en/lexicon/machinic-phylum) — Deleuze/Guattari's concept of the lineage of technical problems that transmit by transforming; how innovations propagate across apparently unrelated domains
- [Maintainers](/en/lexicon/maintainers) — Guardians of original projects who decide which contributions to accept or reject; representing the central authority contested by the Heredoc Manifesto
- [Mean Image](/en/lexicon/mean-image)
- [Metastable](/en/lexicon/metastable) — Simondon's concept of a state of dynamic equilibrium containing unresolved tensions; system poised between stability and transformation, capable of individuation — Steyerl's term (2023) playing on "mean" as both mathematical average and cruelty; statistical composites in AI-generated images that erase singularity. Shows how generative models flatten difference into normative representations
- [Melancholy (Structural)](/en/lexicon/melancholy-structural) — Author's term drawing on Freud's distinction from mourning; condition where the lost object (industrial past, modernist promise) continues to structure the present without being able to be rejoined. Eden is sustained by this structural melancholy
- [Meliorism](/en/lexicon/meliorism) — Pragmatist conviction that human effort might improve conditions; Dewey's driving faith that intelligent action can address problems. Author argues meliorism "spins in a void" when capital has withdrawn the substrate for improvement
- [Model Collapse](/en/lexicon/model-collapse) — Shumailov et al.'s term (Nature, 2024) for degenerative process when generative models train recursively on synthetic data; causes irreversible loss of distributional tails and minority information. Also called "Habsburg AI" or "AI inbreeding"
- [Monotechnics](/en/lexicon/monotechnics) — Single technological paradigm imposed as universal
- [Musée Précaire Albinet](/en/lexicon/musee-precaire-albinet) — Hirschhorn's temporary museum project in Aubervilliers (2004)
- [Moten, Fred](/en/lexicon/moten-fred) — American poet and theorist; undercommons and Black study
- [Maintenance Art](/en/lexicon/maintenance-art) — Ukeles's concept declaring care work as art
- [Manning, Erin](/en/lexicon/manning-erin) — Canadian philosopher; movement and research-creation
- [Massumi, Brian](/en/lexicon/massumi-brian) — Canadian philosopher; affect and process philosophy
- [Mbembe, Achille](/en/lexicon/mbembe-achille) — Cameroonian philosopher; necropolitics
- [Mesh Network](/en/lexicon/mesh-network) — Decentralized network topology; nodes relay data peer-to-peer
- [Minor Gesture](/en/lexicon/minor-gesture) — Manning's concept for catalytic gestures altering the field

## N

- [Nachleben](/en/lexicon/nachleben) — Warburg's concept of "afterlife" or "survival" of images and forms; how visual formulas persist and transform across historical periods, reactivated in new contexts
- [Negri, Antonio](/en/lexicon/negri-antonio) — Italian Marxist philosopher; Empire, multitude, and autonomism
- [Negentropic Obligation](/en/lexicon/negentropic-obligation) — Author's ethical term extending Stiegler's negentropy (Neganthropocene); where Stiegler theorizes negentropy as life's resistance to disorder, Author formulates the duty to maintain difference and singularity against homogenizing entropy
- [Neuronal Paradigm](/en/lexicon/neuronal-paradigm) — Author's term (after Busta) for the shift from linear to network-based cultural transmission; contents circulate by contagion, lateral connections replace vertical transmission, AI systems make paradigm visible
- [New Aesthetic](/en/lexicon/new-aesthetic) — Bridle's concept (SXSW 2012) describing the eruption of the digital into the physical world: glitches, pixelation, surveillance imagery becoming visible in everyday life

## O

- [Opacity](/en/lexicon/opacity) — Glissant's concept of the right not to be understood
- [Optical Unconscious](/en/lexicon/optical-unconscious) — Benjamin's concept of what photography reveals beyond intention
- [Olson, Charles](/en/lexicon/olson-charles) — American poet, rector of BMC; projective verse
- [Ostrom, Elinor](/en/lexicon/ostrom-elinor) — American political economist; commons governance
- [Overfitting](/en/lexicon/overfitting) — Model memorizing training data instead of learning generalizable patterns

## P

- [Parameter / Weight](/en/lexicon/parameter-weight) — Adjustable floating-point number constituting neural network "memory"; encodes complex statistical correlations, the physical materialization of statistical commons
- [Parisi, Luciana](/en/lexicon/parisi-luciana) — Italian philosopher (1967-); computation, abduction, and critical computation
- [Peer-to-Peer (P2P)](/en/lexicon/peer-to-peer) — Decentralized architecture where nodes share resources directly
- [Pharmacological](/en/lexicon/pharmacological) — Stiegler's concept of technology as both poison and cure
- [Pluriverse](/en/lexicon/pluriverse) — Multiple coexisting cosmologies vs. single universe
- [Powers of the False](/en/lexicon/powers-of-the-false) — Deleuze's concept of fabulation producing narratives that relaunch reality; falsity as creative force rather than mere negation of truth
- [Poor Image](/en/lexicon/poor-image)
- [Pre-enactment](/en/lexicon/pre-enactment) — Lütticken's concept of staging futures not yet taken place; shift from reenactment (restaging past) to anticipatory performance of unrealized possibilities
- [Pre-individual](/en/lexicon/pre-individual) — Simondon's concept of the ontological domain prior to individuation; field of potentials from which individuals emerge while never exhausting it
- [Prehension](/en/lexicon/prehension) — Whitehead's concept of how actual entities grasp or feel other entities; expanded perception beyond human consciousness to all relations — Steyerl's concept (2009) of degraded, compressed, copied images that circulate freely; low resolution as condition of accessibility and democratic distribution, opposing the "rich image" of institutional custody
- [Post-Indexical Image](/en/lexicon/post-indexical-image) — Author's term for images without indexical structure; neither "fake" (implying a true original) nor "synthetic" (implying artificiality), but operating in a different ontological register altogether
- [Prismatic Archive](/en/lexicon/prismatic-archive) — Author's term opposing both "dissolved" archive (lost to entropy) and "frozen" archive (fixed in time); archive that diffracts rather than dissolves, producing spectra rather than unities
- [Prismatic Method](/en/lexicon/prismatic-method) — Author's term for refraction of heterogeneous frameworks revealing frequencies none could render visible alone; distinct from synthesis, which merges; prism separates and recombines
- [Problem Situation](/en/lexicon/problem-situation) — Dewey's concept of the indeterminate situation requiring intelligent action; inquiry begins not from doubt but from felt difficulty demanding resolution
- [Procedural Generation](/en/lexicon/procedural-generation) — Algorithmic generation of content through rules rather than manual creation; variation within constraint, anticipating AI generation
- [Proletarianization (of the Mind)](/en/lexicon/proletarianization-of-the-mind)
- [Proprioceptive Poetics](/en/lexicon/proprioceptive-poetics) — Olson's concept of poetry emphasizing bodily knowledge and breath; verse as extension of the poet's physiology onto the page
- [Proto-computational](/en/lexicon/proto-computational) — Author's term for structures anticipating computational aesthetics without computational machinery; BMC practices (weaving, chance operations, feedback) as pre-digital computation — Stiegler's concept of knowledge loss through automation
- [Prompt Engineering](/en/lexicon/prompt-engineering) — The art of formulating optimal instructions to obtain results from an LLM; the prompt as "new literacy" and "incantation" addressed to compressed collective intelligence
- [Pull Request](/en/lexicon/pull-request) — Modification proposal in software development: "Here are the improvements I made in my fork, would you like to integrate them into your version?"
- [Pragmatism](/en/lexicon/pragmatism) — American philosophical tradition (Peirce, James, Dewey) emphasizing experience, inquiry, and consequences over abstract truth. BMC's theoretical foundation; Author argues its meliorist assumptions require critique under exocapitalist conditions
- [Presence and Production](/en/lexicon/presence-and-production) — Hirschhorn's twin principles: physical presence and active making on-site
- [Pirate Care](/en/lexicon/pirate-care) — Care practices operating outside legal frameworks
- [Plant, Sadie](/en/lexicon/plant-sadie) — British theorist; cyberfeminism and Zeros and Ones
- [Post-Documentary](/en/lexicon/post-documentary) — *Author's term* for practices after collapse of indexical certainty
- [Porras-Kim, Gala](/en/lexicon/porras-kim-gala) — Colombian-Korean American artist; speculative conservation
- [Potential History](/en/lexicon/potential-history) — Azoulay's concept for recovering foreclosed possibilities
- [Phillips, Rasheedah](/en/lexicon/phillips-rasheedah) — American artist; Black Quantum Futurism

## Q

- [Quasi-causal Technique](/en/lexicon/quasi-causal-technique) — Massumi's concept of gesture creating conditions for event rather than mechanically producing it; enabling rather than determining
- [Quasi-object](/en/lexicon/quasi-object) — Latour's concept of hybrid entities belonging to no single discipline; circulating between human and non-human, nature and culture

## R

- [Reactivation](/en/lexicon/reactivation) — Making latent archival possibilities available for present use
- [Reenactment](/en/lexicon/reenactment) — Lütticken's concept of re-staging past gestures under altered conditions; not faithful reproduction but revelation of what the original contained without knowing it. De Beyter's limousine and Gaignard's still as reenactments of industrial endings
- [Recursive Archive](/en/lexicon/recursive-archive) — Author's term for archive that queries itself; where traditional archives await external interrogation, recursive archives generate questions about their own conditions and gaps
- [Repository](/en/lexicon/repository) — Digital space where a software project lives: a library containing all files, the history of modifications, and the project's rules
- [Retcon](/en/lexicon/retcon) — Retroactive continuity as creative-critical method
- [Rhizome](/en/lexicon/rhizome) — Deleuze/Guattari's concept (A Thousand Plateaus) materialized through technical architecture: structure without fixed center or hierarchy, where knowledge circulates between equal peers
- [Ritornello](/en/lexicon/ritornello) — Deleuze/Guattari's concept of the refrain; gesture creating territory through repetition with difference, establishing home while enabling departure
- [Raad, Walid](/en/lexicon/raad-walid) — Lebanese-American artist; Atlas Group and archival fabulation
- [Rancière, Jacques](/en/lexicon/ranciere-jacques) — French philosopher; equality of intelligences and emancipation
- [Rauschenberg, Robert](/en/lexicon/rauschenberg-robert) — American artist at BMC; Combines and gap between art/life
- [Rogue Archivist](/en/lexicon/rogue-archivist) — *Author's term* for archival practice outside institutional authorization
- [Relational Aesthetics](/en/lexicon/relational-aesthetics) — Bourriaud's term for art creating social situations
- [Rivera Cusicanqui, Silvia](/en/lexicon/rivera-cusicanqui-silvia) — Bolivian sociologist (1949-); ch'ixi and epistemic extractivism
- [RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)](/en/lexicon/rlhf) — Training technique aligning models with human preferences
- [Rouvroy, Antoinette](/en/lexicon/rouvroy-antoinette) — Belgian legal scholar (1971-); algorithmic governmentality

## S

- [Sampling](/en/lexicon/sampling) — Theoretical transposition of sonic practice to epistemology; extracting field of potential from existing materials, citation as material transformation
- [Situated Knowledge](/en/lexicon/situated-knowledge) — Haraway's concept of knowledge from embodied position
- [Srnicek, Nick](/en/lexicon/srnicek-nick) — Canadian theorist; platform capitalism and left accelerationism
- [Sourced Speculation](/en/lexicon/sourced-speculation) — Author's term for imaginative extension grounded in archival evidence; speculation that amplifies rather than fabricates, maintaining accountability to sources while exploring unrealized possibilities
- [Sedimented Tactic](/en/lexicon/sedimented-tactic) — Author's term for tactic with duration and accumulated knowledge without becoming strategy; retains tactical flexibility while building on prior iterations
- [Serial Endosymbiosis](/en/lexicon/serial-endosymbiosis) — Margulis's theory of evolution through incorporation rather than competition; organisms evolve by integrating former parasites as organelles. Model for AI-human symbiosis
- [Spectral Image](/en/lexicon/spectral-image) — Author's term for images produced from statistical distributions; neither present nor absent, haunting the archive without indexical anchor
- [Speculative Archive](/en/lexicon/speculative-archive) — Author's term for archive that projects forward rather than merely preserving backward; generates possible futures from documentary traces rather than fixing the past
- [Statistical Commons](/en/lexicon/statistical-commons)
- [Stochastic Process](/en/lexicon/stochastic-process) — Random process governed by probability laws; mathematical foundation for generative AI, chance constrained by distribution
- [Synergetics](/en/lexicon/synergetics) — Fuller's systems thinking emphasizing geometric and energetic principles; whole-systems approach anticipating network theory — Author's term for shared probability distributions produced by AI training; commons created not through intentional sharing but through mechanical aggregation of human expression into collective statistical patterns
- [Statistical Unconscious](/en/lexicon/statistical-unconscious) — Schröter's term (2023) transposing Benjamin's Optical Unconscious to AI image generation; what machine learning reveals in aggregate patterns that escapes conscious human perception. Where Benjamin's camera revealed hidden gestures, AI reveals hidden distributions
- [Statistics](/en/lexicon/statistics) — Sciences of data analysis and interpretation (etym. "sciences of the State"); in AI context, emphasizes that knowledge emerges through sedimentation and probabilistic recombination
- [Surveillance Capitalism](/en/lexicon/surveillance-capitalism) — Zuboff's concept of economy based on behavioral prediction
- [Symbiotic Algorithm](/en/lexicon/symbiotic-algorithm) — Author's term opposing extractive computation; algorithm as relation rather than extraction, mutual transformation of human and machine
- [Salane, Rose](/en/lexicon/salane-rose) — American artist; archival materials and institutional loss
- [Samizdat](/en/lexicon/samizdat) — Soviet-era clandestine copying and distribution networks
- [Shadow Library](/en/lexicon/shadow-library) — Informal digital repositories providing free access to texts
- [Stoler, Ann Laura](/en/lexicon/stoler-ann-laura) — American anthropologist; archival grain and colonial archives
- [Syjuco, Stephanie](/en/lexicon/syjuco-stephanie) — Filipino-American artist; colonial archives and rogue archiving
- [Systems Aesthetics](/en/lexicon/systems-aesthetics) — Burnham's concept for art emphasizing processes over objects
- [Sholette, Gregory](/en/lexicon/sholette-gregory) — American artist and theorist; dark matter and art labor
- [Simondon, Gilbert](/en/lexicon/simondon-gilbert) — French philosopher (1924-1989); individuation and technical objects
- [Stengers, Isabelle](/en/lexicon/stengers-isabelle) — Belgian philosopher; cosmopolitics
- [Stiegler, Bernard](/en/lexicon/stiegler-bernard) — French philosopher (1952-2020); tertiary retention and pharmacology

## T

- [Tacit Knowledge](/en/lexicon/tacit-knowledge) — Polanyi's concept of knowledge that cannot be fully articulated
- [Tactical Urgency](/en/lexicon/tactical-urgency) — Author's term combining de Certeau's tactics (The Practice of Everyday Life) with temporal pressure; time constraint as strategic resource rather than limitation, generating invention through compression
- [Technical Culture](/en/lexicon/technical-culture)
- [Temperature](/en/lexicon/temperature) — Parameter controlling randomness in AI text generation; higher = more creative
- [Tensegrity](/en/lexicon/tensegrity) — Fuller/Snelson's concept of structural integrity through continuous tension and discontinuous compression; isolated components held in relation by tensional network
- [That-could-have-been](/en/lexicon/that-could-have-been) — Author's term for generated images as projections of statistical plausibility; spectral counterpart to Barthes's "that-has-been," without indexical substance — Simondon's concept of literacy about technical objects
- [Technodiversity](/en/lexicon/technodiversity) — Hui's concept of multiple technical traditions
- [Tertiary Retention](/en/lexicon/tertiary-retention) — Stiegler's concept of externalized technical memory
- [Tertiary Protention](/en/lexicon/tertiary-protention) — Hui's concept (2016) supplementing Stiegler's tertiary retention; where Stiegler theorized externalized memory (retention), Hui adds externalized anticipation—technologies that structure futurity, from debt to algorithmic prediction
- [Token / Tokenization](/en/lexicon/token-tokenization) — Process of fragmenting text or code into elementary units that algorithms manipulate mathematically; the first step in dissolving semantic integrity
- [Training](/en/lexicon/training) — Process by which models learn by iteratively adjusting billions of weights to minimize error; emphasizes the massive ecological cost of this knowledge transformation
- [Transduction](/en/lexicon/transduction) — Simondon's concept of propagating transformation
- [Transformer](/en/lexicon/transformer) — Neural network architecture (2017) that made modern LLMs possible through the attention mechanism; accelerating compression and recombination
- [Transindividuation](/en/lexicon/transindividuation) — Stiegler's concept of individual-collective co-constitution
- [Theater Piece No. 1](/en/lexicon/theater-piece-no-1) — Cage's 1952 BMC event, first "happening"

## U

- [Unboxing](/en/lexicon/unboxing) — Author's theoretical term (distinct from consumer "unboxing" videos); opening Latour's black box to reveal internal complexity, but emphasizing the pedagogical act of exposure rather than mere technical analysis
- [Universe of Technical Images](/en/lexicon/universe-of-technical-images) — Flusser's concept of autonomous image realm
- [Unshared Authorship](/en/lexicon/unshared-authorship) — Hirschhorn's concept of singular authorship enabling collective participation
- [Ukeles, Mierle Laderman](/en/lexicon/ukeles-mierle-laderman) — American artist; maintenance art and care work
- [Undercommons](/en/lexicon/undercommons) — Harney/Moten's concept for fugitive spaces of study

## V

- [Vertical Work](/en/lexicon/vertical-work) — Author's term opposing "horizontal" connection (networking, linking, sharing); deep engagement with singular problems rather than lateral movement across surfaces. Resonates with Stiegler's critique of horizontal proletarianization
- [Virilio, Paul](/en/lexicon/virilio-paul) — French theorist; dromology and speed theory
- [Vibe Coding](/en/lexicon/vibe-coding) — Karpathy's term (February 2025) for AI-assisted programming where developers "forget that the code even exists"; extends his 2023 claim that "the hottest new programming language is English." Marks shift from writing to prompting
- [Visible Labor](/en/lexicon/visible-labor) — Author's inversion of "invisible labor" (Daniels 1987, Terranova's Free Labor); showing process rather than finished results, making pedagogical and creative work apparent rather than concealed
- [VanDerBeek, Stan](/en/lexicon/vanderbeek-stan) — American filmmaker at BMC; computational cinema
- [Void Detection](/en/lexicon/void-detection) — *Author's term* for archival method locating significance in absence

## W

- [Worlding](/en/lexicon/worlding) — Whitehead/Manning's concept of creating space through movement rather than occupying pre-existing space; world as ongoing process rather than container
- [Wayfaring](/en/lexicon/wayfaring) — Ingold's concept of exploratory walk that engenders path by walking; knowledge through movement rather than representation
