Mars and Medak's term for the practice of maintaining knowledge commons without institutional authorization. The custodian differs from the curator (who selects) and the archivist (who preserves according to protocols): custodianship involves active care for shared resources, often in legally precarious conditions. Shadow library maintainers are custodians; so are those who preserve endangered software, digitize out-of-print texts, or maintain decaying infrastructure. Custodianship as ethical stance: taking responsibility for what institutions cannot or will not maintain.
