Deleuze/Guattari's concept of the lineage of technical problems that transmit by transforming. How innovations propagate across apparently unrelated domains—the same "abstract machine" appearing in metallurgy, music, computation. The machinic phylum is not historical influence but transversal connection: BMC's practices (chance operations, feedback, grid structures) belong to the same phylum as digital computation without deriving from it.

**References:**
- DELEUZE, Gilles, and Félix GUATTARI. *A Thousand Plateaus*. Trans. Brian Massumi. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1987.
