Canadian political theorist. With Alex Williams, authored the "Accelerate Manifesto" (2013) and *Inventing the Future: Postcapitalism and a World Without Work* (2015), articulating left accelerationism: not escape from capitalism but repurposing its technologies toward post-capitalist ends. *Platform Capitalism* (2016) analyzes digital platforms as new form of capital accumulation. Against localist "folk politics"; for ambitious counter-hegemonic projects that match capital's scale and ambition.

**Key Concepts:** [[platform-capitalism|Platform Capitalism]], Left Accelerationism, Post-Work
