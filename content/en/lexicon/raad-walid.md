Lebanese-American artist (b. 1967) whose Atlas Group project (1989-2004) created a fictional archive of the Lebanese civil wars. Documents appear authentic but are fabricated, raising questions about archival truth, historical trauma, and the limits of representation. Neither hoax nor straightforward fiction: the work operates in the space where evidence and imagination become indistinguishable. Paradigmatic for Author's concept of "sourced speculation" and the post-indexical condition of archives.
