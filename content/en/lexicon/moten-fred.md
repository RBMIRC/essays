American poet and theorist. With Stefano Harney, developed the concept of the "undercommons" in *The Undercommons: Fugitive Planning and Black Study* (2013)—spaces of study and sociality that exist beneath and against institutional capture. "Study" names a practice of collective thinking that refuses the university's disciplinary divisions. Against professionalization and credentialing; for the surround, the informal, the fugitive. Moten's poetics enact what his theory describes: thought as improvisation, citation as riff.
