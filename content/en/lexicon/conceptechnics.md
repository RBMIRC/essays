Eshun's fusion of concept and sonic technique; sampling as mode of thought where citation becomes material transformation rather than mere reference. Conceptechnics dissolves the distinction between thinking and making: the concept is the technique, the technique thinks. Applied to research-creation: methodology not as frame applied to practice but as practice that generates its own conceptual apparatus.

**References:**
- ESHUN, Kodwo. *More Brilliant than the Sun: Adventures in Sonic Fiction*. London: Quartet Books, 1998.
