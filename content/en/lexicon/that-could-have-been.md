*Author's term* for generated images as projections of statistical plausibility. Spectral counterpart to Barthes's "that-has-been" (ça-a-été), which names photography's testimony to past presence. The AI-generated image testifies to nothing that was—only to what the model's training distribution makes possible. Neither true nor false but plausible: that-could-have-been without ever having been.

**References:**
- Developed in "Differential Automation" (this corpus).
- BARTHES, Roland. *Camera Lucida*. Trans. Richard Howard. New York: Hill and Wang, 1981.
