Warburg's concept of "afterlife" or "survival" of images and forms; how visual formulas (*Pathosformeln*) persist and transform across historical periods, reactivated in new contexts. Images do not simply influence later images—they survive in them, carrying affective charges across time. Foundational for understanding how BMC's gestures persist in the Eden project: not as influence or reference but as survival, reactivated under altered conditions. The *retcon* methodology formalizes this Warburgian insight.

**References:**
- WARBURG, Aby. *The Renewal of Pagan Antiquity*. Trans. David Britt. Los Angeles: Getty Research Institute, 1999.
- DIDI-HUBERMAN, Georges. *L'Image survivante: Histoire de l'art et temps des fantômes selon Aby Warburg*. Paris: Minuit, 2002.
