Dewey's concept of the indeterminate situation requiring intelligent action. Inquiry begins not from abstract doubt but from felt difficulty demanding resolution. The "problem" is not in the mind but in the situation: environment and organism are out of joint, and thinking is the process of restoring coordinated action. Every workshop begins from a problem situation.
