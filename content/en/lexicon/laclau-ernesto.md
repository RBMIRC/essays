Argentine political theorist (1935-2014). With Chantal Mouffe, *Hegemony and Socialist Strategy* (1985) founding text of post-Marxism: rejected class essentialism, proposed discourse theory of political identity. *On Populist Reason* (2005) theorizes populism not as pathology but as logic of political articulation—construction of "the people" against "the elite." Influential on left populist movements (Podemos, Syriza). Key insight: political identities are constructed through antagonism, not discovered in social structure.

**Key Concepts:** Hegemony, Empty Signifier, Populism, Antagonism
