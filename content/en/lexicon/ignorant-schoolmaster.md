Rancière's concept (via Joseph Jacotot) of the teacher who teaches what they do not know, thereby demonstrating the equality of intelligences. The ignorant schoolmaster does not transmit knowledge but verifies that the student has applied their intelligence. Against the "stultifying" model where the master explains and the student receives, Jacotot's method assumes all intelligence is equal and demands the student find their own path. Foundational for contemporary emancipatory pedagogy.
