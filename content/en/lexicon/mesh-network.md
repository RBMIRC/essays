Network topology where nodes connect to multiple other nodes, creating redundant paths. If one node fails, traffic routes around it. Community mesh networks (Guifi.net, NYC Mesh) provide internet access independent of ISPs. Tensegrity principle applied to communication: strength through distributed connection rather than central hub. Relevant to resilient knowledge infrastructure: mesh architectures resist both technical failure and political censorship.
