French sociologist (1947-2022) whose actor-network theory and concept of the black box inform analysis of technical systems and their social entanglements. Key concepts include [[Black Box]], [[Actor-Network Theory]], and [[Inscription]].

**Key Works:** *Science in Action* (1987), *Reassembling the Social* (2005)
