Processor optimized for parallel computation, originally for graphics rendering. Matrix operations central to deep learning map efficiently onto GPU architecture. NVIDIA's dominance in AI GPUs creates supply chain dependencies and geopolitical tensions. Training large models requires thousands of GPUs running for months—massive energy consumption and capital investment. The material substrate of AI: rare earth mining, fabrication plants, data center cooling. "The Cloud" is other people's GPUs.
