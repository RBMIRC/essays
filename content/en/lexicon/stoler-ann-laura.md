American anthropologist and historian specializing in colonial archives. *Along the Archival Grain* (2009) reads colonial archives "along the grain"—attending to their internal logic, anxieties, and contradictions rather than simply mining them for facts. Demonstrates that archives reveal as much about colonial administrators' epistemic uncertainties as about colonized populations. Concept of "epistemic anxieties" shows how imperial knowledge was always partial, contested, and shot through with doubt.

**Key Concepts:** Archival Grain, Epistemic Anxieties, Colonial Intimacies
