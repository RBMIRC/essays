Guattari's concept of oscillation between chaos and emergence of provisional cosmos. Creative process navigating between complete disorder (which produces nothing) and over-determined structure (which reproduces the same). Chaosmosis names the zone where novelty becomes possible: enough constraint to cohere, enough chaos to transform. The workshop operates chaosmodically: structured enough to produce, open enough to surprise.

**References:**
- GUATTARI, Félix. *Chaosmosis: An Ethico-Aesthetic Paradigm*. Trans. Paul Bains and Julian Pefanis. Bloomington: Indiana University Press, 1995.
