Belgian political theorist (b. 1943). With Ernesto Laclau, developed post-Marxist theory in *Hegemony and Socialist Strategy* (1985). *The Democratic Paradox* (2000) and *Agonistics* (2013) propose "agonistic pluralism": democratic politics requires adversaries, not enemies—conflict channeled through institutions rather than eliminated. Critique of deliberative democracy's consensualism (Habermas, Rawls). Politics as constitutively conflictual; the task is to transform antagonism (us/them as enemies) into agonism (us/them as adversaries).

**Key Concepts:** Agonism, Hegemony, Radical Democracy
