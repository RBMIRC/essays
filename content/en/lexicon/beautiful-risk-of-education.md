Biesta's concept defending education's inherent uncertainty against the demand for measurable outcomes. Education involves risk because it addresses subjects who may respond in unpredictable ways—this unpredictability is not a flaw to be eliminated but a condition of genuine education. The "weakness" of education (its inability to guarantee results) is also its strength: it treats students as subjects rather than objects to be molded. Against "learnification" and the culture of control.

**References:**
- BIESTA, Gert. *The Beautiful Risk of Education*. Boulder: Paradigm Publishers, 2013.
- BIESTA, Gert. "Against Learning: Reclaiming a Language for Education in an Age of Learning." *Nordisk Pedagogik* 25 (2005): 54-66.
