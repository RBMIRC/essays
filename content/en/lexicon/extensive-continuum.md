title: "Extensive Continuum"
type: glossary
aliases: []
tags:

---

Whitehead's concept of the relational field underlying all actual occasions; potentiality for connection prior to any particular actualization. Not empty space but the condition for spatial and temporal relations to emerge. The extensive continuum is "the most general scheme of real potentiality"—what makes relation possible before any specific relation occurs.

**References:**
- WHITEHEAD, Alfred North. *Process and Reality*. New York: Free Press, 1929/1978.
- STENGERS, Isabelle. *Thinking with Whitehead*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2011.
