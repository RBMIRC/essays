Hirschhorn's temporary museum project (2004) in the Quartier du Landy, Aubervilliers—a working-class suburb of Paris. For eight weeks, borrowed masterworks from Centre Pompidou and other institutions were displayed in a makeshift structure built with residents. Exemplifies "presence and production": Hirschhorn lived nearby, worked daily on-site, and the project included workshops, meals, and discussions. Not art about community but art with community through sustained physical co-presence.
