British cultural theorist (1968-2017) whose concepts of hauntology and lost futures inform the project's approach to archival reactivation and speculative historiography. Key concepts include [[Capitalist Realism]], [[Hauntology]], and [[Lost Futures]].

**Key Works:** *Capitalist Realism* (2009), *Ghosts of My Life* (2014)
