Walid Raad's artistic project (1989-2004) creating a fictional archive of the Lebanese civil wars. Documents include fabricated photographs, forged notebooks, and invented testimonies—presented with archival apparatus (finding aids, accession numbers) that mimics institutional authenticity. Neither hoax nor documentary: work operates in the gap where the distinction between evidence and imagination collapses. Paradigmatic for Author's concepts of "sourced speculation" and the post-indexical archive.
