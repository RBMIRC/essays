American artist (b. 1939) who declared all domestic and maintenance work as art in "Manifesto for Maintenance Art" (1969). Since 1977, unsalaried Artist-in-Residence at New York City Department of Sanitation. *Touch Sanitation* (1979-1980) involved shaking hands with all 8,500 sanitation workers. Work makes visible the labor that sustains social life but remains systematically invisible. Foundational for social practice and for understanding care and maintenance as constitutive of any commons—including knowledge commons.

**Key Concepts:** Maintenance Art, Care Work, Social Sculpture
