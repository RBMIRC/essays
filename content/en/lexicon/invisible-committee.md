Anonymous French collective emerging from Tiqqun journal (1999-2001). *The Coming Insurrection* (2007) diagnosed social decomposition and proposed communal forms beyond work and state. *To Our Friends* (2014) reflected on post-2008 insurrections; *Now* (2017) continued tactical analysis. Develops concept of "insurrectionary we" constituted through intensity of shared practice rather than identity. Tarnac affair (2008) brought state attention. Influential on contemporary commune theory and direct action.

**Key Concepts:** Insurrectionary We, Commune, Destituent Power
