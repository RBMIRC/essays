Ann Laura Stoler's method of reading colonial archives "along the grain"—attending to their internal logic, anxieties, and categories rather than simply extracting facts. The grain reveals what archives were designed to do, what worried their creators, how classifications were constructed and contested. Reading along the grain exposes archives as sites of uncertainty rather than confident knowledge: colonial administrators doubted, feared, and improvised. Method for understanding archives as active instruments rather than passive repositories.
