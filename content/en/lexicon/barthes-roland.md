French literary theorist and philosopher (1915-1980) whose work on photography in *Camera Lucida* provides foundational concepts for understanding the ontological transformation of the image in the age of AI. Key concepts include [[Punctum]], [[Studium]], [[Ça-a-été]], and [[Adherent Referent]].

**Key Works:** *Camera Lucida* (1980), *Mythologies* (1957), *S/Z* (1970)
