Bolivian sociologist (1949-) whose Aymara concept of ch'ixi offers an alternative to Western dialectics for thinking contradiction without resolution. Key concepts include [[Ch'ixi]] and [[Epistemic Extractivism]].

**Key Works:** *Ch'ixinakax utxiwa* (2010)
