Belgian visual artist whose work investigates post-industrial landscapes and collective memory. *Limousine* (2018) documents the reconstruction of a Lincoln stretch limousine in a former industrial site in Eden—the car functioning as spectral presence of American aspirations in deindustrialized Europe. Work resonates with Author's interest in reenactment as method and the structural melancholy of abandoned industrial promise. The luxury vehicle as both object and symbol of what was promised and withdrawn.
