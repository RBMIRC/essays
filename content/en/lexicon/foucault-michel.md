French philosopher (1926-1984) whose concept of the archive as a system of rules determining what can be said provides the critical framework for analyzing AI's transformation of archival ontology. Key concepts include [[Archive]], [[Apparatus/Dispositif]], [[Discourse]], and [[Power/Knowledge]].

**Key Works:** *The Archaeology of Knowledge* (1969), *Discipline and Punish* (1975)
