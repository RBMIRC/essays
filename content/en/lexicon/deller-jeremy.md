British artist (b. 1966) whose work often involves reenactment and collective participation. *The Battle of Orgreave* (2001) restaged the 1984 miners' strike confrontation with original participants and historical reenactors, collapsing the distinction between documentary and performance. *Acid Brass* (1997) had a traditional brass band perform acid house tracks, staging cultural collision as method. Work demonstrates reenactment as both historical investigation and social sculpture, relevant to Author's interest in "making the past available for the future."
