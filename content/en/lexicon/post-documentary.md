*Author's term* for artistic and archival practices that operate after the collapse of indexical certainty. Where documentary assumed a stable relationship between image and referent (Barthes's "that-has-been"), post-documentary works in the space where this relationship has dissolved. Not anti-documentary (which would still reference what it negates) but a different epistemic regime altogether. Includes practices of sourced speculation, hysterical documentation, and statistical image generation.
