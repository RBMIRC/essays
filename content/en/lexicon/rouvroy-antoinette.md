Belgian legal scholar (1971-) whose concept of algorithmic governmentality describes the mode of power operating through statistical prediction rather than legal prohibition. Key concepts include [[Algorithmic Governmentality]] and [[Data Behaviorism]].

**Key Works:** Various articles on algorithmic governance (2010-2013)
