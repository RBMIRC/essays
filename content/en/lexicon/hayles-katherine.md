American literary critic and media theorist. *How We Became Posthuman* (1999) traced cybernetics' erasure of embodiment; *Writing Machines* (2002) developed "media-specific analysis" attending to material substrates; *Unthought* (2017) theorizes nonconscious cognition in technical systems. Key insight: meaning emerges from interaction between signifier, signifying system, and material conditions of production. Against disembodied information; for attention to how media shape what can be thought.

**Key Concepts:** Media-Specific Analysis, Technogenesis, Nonconscious Cognition
