German philosopher (1892-1940) whose analysis of photography's revelation of the 'optical unconscious' provides historical context for understanding AI's 'statistical unconscious.' Key concepts include [[Optical Unconscious]], [[Aura]], and [[Mechanical Reproduction]].

**Key Works:** *The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction* (1935)
