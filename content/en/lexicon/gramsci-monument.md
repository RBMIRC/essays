Hirschhorn's project at Forest Houses public housing in the South Bronx (summer 2013), commissioned by Dia Art Foundation. A temporary monument to Antonio Gramsci comprising library, lecture hall, radio station, and workshop spaces—built with and programmed by residents over 77 days. Hirschhorn maintained daily presence; programming included philosophy discussions, poetry readings, computer classes. Demonstrates "unshared authorship": Hirschhorn authors the structure while residents determine its use and meaning.
