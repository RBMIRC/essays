British cultural theorist and philosopher, co-founder of the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit (CCRU) at Warwick with Nick Land. Pioneer of cyberfeminism and theorist of digital culture. Her work *Zeros and Ones: Digital Women and the New Technoculture* (1997) rewrites computing history through Ada Lovelace and weaving, arguing that women and computing have always been intertwined—the binary code itself feminine, the loom a proto-computer. Challenges masculinist narratives of technological development. Also author of *Writing on Drugs* (1999) exploring the relationship between psychoactive substances and culture. Her work prefigures contemporary discussions of gender, technology, and posthumanism.

- PLANT, Sadie. *Zeros and Ones: Digital Women and the New Technoculture*. London: Fourth Estate, 1997.
