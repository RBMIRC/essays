British anthropologist whose work bridges social anthropology, archaeology, art, and architecture. Key concepts include "education of attention" (learning through directed perception), "wayfaring" (knowledge generated through movement), and "making" as correspondence with materials rather than imposition of form. *Lines* (2007) and *Making* (2013) theorize creativity as improvisation within fields of relations rather than execution of plans. Critique of "hylomorphism"—the assumption that form is imposed on matter—resonates with Author's emphasis on material agency.
