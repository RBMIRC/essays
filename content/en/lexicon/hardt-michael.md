American political theorist (b. 1960). With Antonio Negri, authored the *Empire* trilogy analyzing global capitalism and revolutionary potential. *Assembly* (2017) addresses movements' organizational challenges. Translates Italian autonomist thought into American academy. Key contribution: theorizing "affective labor" and "immaterial labor" as increasingly central to production under cognitive capitalism—and therefore as potential sites of resistance.

**Key Concepts:** Empire, Affective Labor, Assembly
