French sociologist and philosopher (1929-2007). Theorist of simulation, hyperreality, and the "precession of simulacra." *Simulacra and Simulation* (1981) argues we live in a world of copies without originals—the map precedes the territory. *The Gulf War Did Not Take Place* (1991) provocatively claimed the media spectacle replaced the event. Early work (*The System of Objects*, 1968; *Consumer Society*, 1970) analyzed consumer capitalism. Relevant to post-indexical images: Baudrillard anticipated a regime where representation detaches entirely from referent.

**Key Concepts:** Simulacra, Hyperreality, Implosion, Symbolic Exchange
