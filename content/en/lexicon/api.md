Defined interface allowing software systems to communicate. APIs specify what requests can be made and what responses to expect. OpenAI API, for example, allows applications to send prompts and receive completions. APIs as control points: who has access, at what cost, with what rate limits. The "API-fication" of AI concentrates power in providers who control access. Also enables integration: code can call AI capabilities without understanding internal workings.
