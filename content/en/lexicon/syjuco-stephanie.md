Filipino-American artist (b. 1974) whose work investigates colonial archives, material culture, and the construction of identity. *Cargo Cults* series examines how colonialism circulates through objects and images. "Rogue archivist" practice involves intervening in existing archives rather than creating new ones—revealing what institutional collections exclude or misrepresent. Work demonstrates how artistic practice can denaturalize archival categories and expose the colonial assumptions embedded in museum classification.
