Manning's concept for the space where relational movement occurs—not empty gap but active zone of potential. The interval is where the "elasticity of the almost" operates: sensing possible movements before they occur, feeling what might happen. Not between pre-existing points but generative of the points that seem to bound it. Related to Whitehead's "actual occasions" emerging from relational fields. The workshop as intervallic space: where encounter happens, where becoming unfolds.
