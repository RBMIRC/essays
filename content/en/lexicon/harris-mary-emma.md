American art historian specializing in Black Mountain College. *The Arts at Black Mountain College* (1987) provides comprehensive documentation of BMC's artistic programs based on extensive archival research and interviews. Later work on BMC archives and exhibitions continued documenting the college's legacy. Essential scholarly source for understanding how visual arts, music, dance, and poetry intersected in BMC's experimental environment.
