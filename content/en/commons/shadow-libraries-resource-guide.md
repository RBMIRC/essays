## 1. SHADOW LIBRARIES (ACTIVE PLATFORMS)

### Large-Scale Libraries

| Platform | URL | Founded | Scale | Status |
|----------|-----|---------|-------|--------|
| **Library Genesis (LibGen)** | Mirrors vary (consult Monoskop/Anna's Archive) | 2008 | 2.4M+ books, 80M+ articles | Active, under legal pressure. $30M damages (Sept. 2024). LibGen+ fork active. |
| **Sci-Hub** | Multiple mirrors | 2011 | 80M+ scientific articles | Active. Founded by Alexandra Elbakyan (Kazakhstan). |
| **Anna's Archive** | https://annas-archive.org/ | 2022 | Meta-library aggregating LibGen, Sci-Hub, Z-Library, Open Library, DuXiu | Active. WorldCat scrape (Oct. 2023) added 700M+ records. Open-source code. |
| **Z-Library** | Varies (app-based access) | ~2009 | Millions of books | Seized by FBI (Nov. 2022). Operators arrested. Partially relaunched via app. |

*Note: URLs for large shadow libraries change frequently due to domain seizures. Monoskop and Anna's Archive maintain current mirror lists.*

## 2. PARALLEL & OFFLINE LIBRARIES

| Project | URL / Info | Description | Key Figures |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Bibliotecha** | Open-source framework | Offline local libraries on Raspberry Pi. Creates local WiFi hotspot serving an e-book catalog. | Andre Castro, Roelof Roscam Abbing, Max Dovey et al. |
| **Pl4tform** | Backpack server | Itinerant digital libraries distributed via portable servers. Embodied colportage. | Leo Leyens |
| **TOUK TOUK BOOK TOUR** | Mobile library | Contemporary colportage through traveling book distribution. | Gaelle Clarck & Joëlle Slacmeulder |
| **Piratebox / LibraryBox** | Open-source | Portable offline file-sharing devices based on router hardware. | David Darts (PirateBox), Jason Griffey (LibraryBox) |

*These projects are particularly relevant for **workshop contexts** — they can be built collectively in a few hours and demonstrate the material infrastructure of knowledge circulation.*
